1. [ ] Confirm the **cohort, teaching week, date, room and lesson duration**. CML3112 normally meets Tuesday 11:00; CML3113 Friday 08:00; CML3111 Friday 11:00. Check the timetable for changes.

3. [ ] Open the cohort's actual student package and complete its main activity from a fresh runtime. Check all necessary datasets are included and the filenames match the instructions. Keep a local fallback copy.
4. [ ] Check the [assignment link index](Assignment_Submission_Links.md): correct cohort, required files, opening time, deadline and whether the assessment is released. A future date alone does not make a held assessment ready.
5. [ ] Read the week's five quiz questions and feedback privately. Confirm the QR/short link points to the correct cohort and week, verified email is required, only one response is allowed and scoring is immediate. Use [the quiz operations guide](IUEA_ML_Weekly_Quiz_Operations_Guide.docx) if needed.
6. [ ] Prepare the message containing the student package, responder links and exact Kampala deadlines. Share only the student material. Do not share Apps Script editors, response spreadsheets, hidden checks, lecturer notes, calibration answers or grade exports.
7. [ ] Check the roster for missing registration numbers, cohort mappings and registered Google emails. Preserve an export/backup of existing marks. Resolve identity mismatches before importing grades.
8. [ ] If grading is planned after class, confirm Docker Desktop and local Ollama are running and the relevant grader/calibration is ready. Follow [assessment operations](grader/README.md). Keep unvalidated assessments and unresolved results pending.

### Thirty minutes before

9. [ ] Test projection, audio if needed, internet, power and demonstration sign-ins. Open the slides, student package, quiz timer and correct owner controls before screen sharing.
10. [ ] Preview the responder links from the separate student account. Check access and the displayed deadline without submitting a real-form test response. Closed-before-opening is expected; recheck when the scheduled window begins.
11. [ ] Prepare a visible agenda and the exact submission instructions. Have the full link available alongside the QR code for students whose cameras fail.

12. [ ] Briefly recap the prerequisite and explain what students will produce today.

15. [ ] Finish with a submission check: correct identity, all required files, Submit clicked, receipt retained, and local copies saved. State the deadline aloud and in writing.
