IN CLASS — FOLLOW THIS ORDER
1. Open proof_of_life.ipynb in Google Colab. This is your MAIN notebook.
   Upload the supplied CSV through Colab's Files sidebar. Follow the lecturer
   as you run cells, enter your own Student Registration Number and investigate
   what happens when a cell runs more than once.
2. When the lecturer starts the repair activity, open the numbered notebooks
   one at a time: 01, then 02, then 03, then 04. These are PRACTICE exercises.
   They deliberately contain errors or incorrect behaviour. Predict what is
   wrong, run, diagnose and repair each one. The lecturer demonstrates 01;
   you attempt 02 and 03 with support; discuss 04 together. Keep your copies.
3. Return to proof_of_life.ipynb. Finish your own execution-order explanation.
   Restart and Run All. Re-upload the CSV if Colab has removed it.
4. Save/download your completed main notebook. Replace STUDENT_ID in the
   filename STUDENT_ID_W02.ipynb with your own Student Registration Number,
   changing slashes to underscores in the FILENAME only.
5. Write your own README.md: state your programme, list your repository files
   and explain how someone else can run your notebook. The supplied README
   files are instructions, not your completed written submission.
