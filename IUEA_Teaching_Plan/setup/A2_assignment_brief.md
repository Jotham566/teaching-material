# A2 — Proof of life: quick reminder

Open **proof_of_life.ipynb**. Its opening instructions give your cohort's CSV, required work and marks.

Submit **two files together**: your completed notebook and your own README.md. Paste your private repository URL in the form. The CSV belongs in your runtime/repository, not the upload form.

Your README must state your programme, exact file list and how to run the notebook. Do not submit the instruction README from the ZIP.

The repairs 01–04, hello_engineering.py and guided_practice.ipynb are practice only.

For help with accounts, GitHub or saving, use the relevant section of STUDENT_HOW_TO.md. Read the live cohort form for the deadline. A1 and the quizzes are separate submissions.
