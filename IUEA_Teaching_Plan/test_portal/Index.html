Open and complete your test

  1. Open Google Colab on your laptop. Sign in with the Google email you entered above.
  2. Choose File → Open notebook → Upload. Select the downloaded . Save a copy in Drive and work in that copy. Click Connect if shown.
  3. Click the folder icon on the left, then Upload (upward arrow). Choose engineering_data.csv and wait until it appears. Uploading the notebook does not upload the CSV.

Submit before the deadline

  1. Save and run your completed cells. If the runtime was restarted, run the supplied setup cell again (or upload your assigned CSV for a manually loaded notebook). Check your work. Submit completed work even if some tasks remain unfinished.
  2. Run the final submission cell. Select your own Google account and review the access prompt. Click Submit test and wait for its form link.
  3. Open that link using the same Google account. Check your details. Leave the automatically filled test allocation code and snapshot fields unchanged. Tick the email confirmation box and press Submit.
  4. Wait for the form confirmation. Keep the saved snapshot unchanged in Drive until marks are released.
