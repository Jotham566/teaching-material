# CML3112 Block 2: what you hand in and when

Block 2 covers Weeks 11 to 13: features and ablation; choosing a model; regularisation, nearest neighbours and reproducibility.

## Assignment

You do the same assignment notebooks as before. They now close together, so you can plan the block as one piece of work.

| Part | Topic | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| A10 (Week 11) | Features and ablation | 20 |
| A11 (Week 12) | Model recommendation | 20 |
| Total | | 40 |

**All parts close:** 7 December 2026, 23:59 Kampala. Each part has its own submission form, listed in the Assignment Submission Links file. A part you do not submit scores zero, so submit every part. You may submit the early parts as soon as you finish them.

## Quiz

One quiz sitting for the whole block: Week 11 quiz (Q10), Week 12 quiz (Q11), Week 13 quiz (Q12). In the last twenty minutes of the Week 13 class the lecturer opens the three quiz forms together; each stays open for 24 hours. Each form is worth 5 marks, so the block quiz is out of 15.

## How it counts

Weeks 1 to 5 stay weekly, and Week 6 is waived. For those weeks your best 3 weekly quizzes and best 3 weekly assignments count. The two blocks always count in full. Quizzes are marked out of 45 and assignments out of 160. The quiz component is worth 10 percent of the course and the assignment component 10 percent.

The guided practice notebook for each week is for practice in class and is not submitted.
