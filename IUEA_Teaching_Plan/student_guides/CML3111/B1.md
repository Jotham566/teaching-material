## How it counts

Your Semester 1 mark out of 100 is: assignments 20 (A1 to A5, each marked out of 20, all five count equally) + quizzes 20 (Q1 to Q5, each marked out of 5, all five count equally) + Practical Test 1 20 + Engineering ML project 40 (Part A 30 + Part B 15 = 45 marks, scaled to 40). There is no best-3-of-5 rule and no Week 6 waiver any more: a missing item scores zero. The Data Analysis project, Practical Test 2 and the demo do not count. The Week 8 to 11 block assignments and quizzes (A7 to A11, Q7 to Q10) and A6 and Q6 are practice and do not count toward the final mark.
