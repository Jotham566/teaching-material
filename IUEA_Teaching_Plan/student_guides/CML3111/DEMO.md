# CML3111 DEMO — Presentation and individual reflection

## Start here

Use your own Student Registration Number exactly as on your university record. Sign in to upload with the Google account registered with the lecturer. If you need account or file help, read STUDENT_HOW_TO.md supplied with the student materials. A repository is a folder of saved project files; an upload form is where you hand in assessed work. Saving to a repository does not submit an assignment.

## Do the work

1. In PowerPoint choose Blank Presentation, or open https://slides.google.com and choose Blank. Use readable slide text to explain your engineering question, method, results and limitations. Add your full spoken explanation in the Notes area beneath each slide.
2. In a separate Word/Google Docs document, include your two individual written-defence answers, referring to specific cells/results in your own project. Use the two prompts issued by the lecturer; do not invent replacement questions.
3. Add a 500-word individual reflection: what changed since Week 1, what remains difficult and two concrete Semester 2 goals. Add your complete AI-use declaration, including none if none was used.
4. Save slides as PPTX (Google Slides: File > Download > Microsoft PowerPoint). Save the written defence/reflection/declaration as DOCX or selectable-text PDF. Reopen both; check narration is still in slide notes.

## Check and submit

Submit the presentation PPTX and one document containing your individual defence, reflection and AI-use declaration together. Include the repository URL and team ID where applicable. The marks assess submitted evidence: presentation 35, individual defence 40, reflection 20, declaration 5.

## How to hand in

Open the correct cohort/assessment form below and read its current deadline. Fill the required identity fields; leave Team ID blank for individual work. Choose Add file > Upload and select the complete required file set. Wait for the filenames to appear, then click Submit. Keep the confirmation page and local copies. Each file must be at most 10 MB. If replacing work before the deadline, send the complete set again; the latest on-time response replaces the earlier response. If the form is closed or upload fails, keep your files and contact the lecturer; do not use a different assignment form.
