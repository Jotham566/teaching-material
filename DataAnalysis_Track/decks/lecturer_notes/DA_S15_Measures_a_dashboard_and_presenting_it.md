DISCUSSION KEY FOR THIS SLIDE
ANSWERS
How did you do?
A
Measure against column
If the answer depends on what the reader filtered, it must be a measure. A column is fixed when the data loads and cannot respond to a slicer.
B
A question you cannot answer
"I do not know, and here is what I would need to find out." It costs you nothing and it protects every other number you gave them. Guessing costs you all of them.
C
Why the answer comes second
Attention is highest at the start and you cannot control when it ends. Build to a conclusion and you may never reach it: somebody interrupts at minute three and the room leaves with your method.
Let learners attempt first; use this to explain the reasoning, not just reveal the answer.
