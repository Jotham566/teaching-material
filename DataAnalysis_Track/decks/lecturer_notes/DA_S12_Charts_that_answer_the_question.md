## Slide 19 — Can you answer these?

TEACHING PURPOSE
Can you answer these?

CONNECT
Build on the preceding slide: Titling a chart 'Revenue by month'. That is the axes read aloud, and it leaves the reader to do the work you were paid to do..

EXPLAIN IN ORDINARY WORDS
A repository is the project folder and recorded history. A commit records a chosen change; uploading or pushing shares it. A README tells someone else how to run the work.

CHECK AND EXPECTED ANSWER
Does saving to GitHub hand in the assignment? No; submission requires the correct form and its confirmation.

IF LEARNERS ARE STUCK
Ask a partner to follow only the README. It should identify the data, files, run order and expected result. Keep secrets out of the repository.

DISCUSSION KEY FOR THIS SLIDE
ANSWERS
How did you do?
A
"Revenue by region" as a title
It is the axes read aloud. It states the method rather than the finding, so the reader has to do the analysis themselves. "Eastern is our largest region, at UGX 2.51bn" is the same chart doing its job.
B
When truncation is legitimate
When zero is not meaningful and the audience knows it: body temperature, an index, a share price over one day. For revenue, volumes or counts, start at zero.
C
What a monthly line hides
The distribution inside each month. One large order can make a month, and the line cannot show you that. The largest single order here is UGX 46,552,400, which is visible in a histogram and invisible in a monthly total.
Let learners attempt first; use this to explain the reasoning, not just reveal the answer.
