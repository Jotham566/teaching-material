## Slide 7 — A statistic

TEACHING PURPOSE
A statistic

CONNECT
Build on the preceding slide: What a statistic is, before any particular one.

LECTURER CUE
One number standing in for many. Every statistic throws information away, which is exactly what makes it useful and exactly what makes it dangerous.

EXPLAIN IN ORDINARY WORDS
An observation describes the data. A finding answers a defined question with evidence. A recommendation adds an action, its justification and limits.

CHECK AND EXPECTED ANSWER
Does a difference between two groups prove one caused the other? No. Other differences or selection may explain it.

IF LEARNERS ARE STUCK
Ask for the population, time period, number and comparison. Then ask what additional evidence could change the recommendation.

DISCUSSION KEY FOR THIS SLIDE
ANSWERS
How did you do?
A
When the mean is right
When the distribution is roughly symmetric, or when you genuinely need the total divided by the count, which is what average order value is for pricing and capacity. As a description of typical, on a skewed distribution, it is wrong.
B
Is the largest order an outlier to delete?
You cannot tell from the data alone, and deleting it is a decision about the business rather than about the file. It might be a typo and it might be your most important customer. Go and ask. Describe it either way.
C
Why look at the shape first
Because the shape is what decides whether a statistic is honest. Choosing the statistic first and looking at the shape afterwards is how the mean ends up in board papers where the median belonged.
Let learners attempt first; use this to explain the reasoning, not just reveal the answer.
