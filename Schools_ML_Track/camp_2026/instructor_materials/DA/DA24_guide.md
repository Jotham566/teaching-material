# DA24 Instructor guide: Parent showcase and next steps

Private. Keep this file out of the student folder.

**Objective.** By the end: you can present your project, answer questions, and try a small change to it live with no AI assistant open.

**Question of the day.** What can I show and explain, and what will I learn next?

**Where this is used.** Start-ups and tech hubs hold demo days where builders show what they made and answer questions. Today is your demo day. What do you want your family to remember about your work?

Prerequisite: DA23 Explain it to a parent.

## The 180 minutes

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 30 | setup, rehearsal and backups |
| 10 | welcome for parents and guardians |
| 40 | presentations and questions |
| 10 | break |
| 20 | feedback, certificates and photographs only where consent exists |
| 70 | reflection, project folder copy and next steps |

Where it overruns: A combined event for several groups needs a separate, longer slot than this plan allows.

## Make: reference

Use the SHOWCASE_GUIDE.md checklist as a guide for friendly feedback. Typical suggested changes: filter one branch, change a threshold from 5 units to 10, add a label to a chart, or rerun a calculation with a different column. The certificate goes to learners who attended or made up most classes, kept their build log and showed a finished project. The camp has no tests and no scores. The programme issues the certificate. No university or vendor qualification is involved.

Build log entry to look for: A showcase talk, one small live change, one strength and one next step, and a certificate decision based on attendance, build log and finished project.

## Misconceptions to listen for

- The showcase is a performance.
- Help must be hidden.
- A certificate certifies a profession.

## Questions to ask, with expected answers

- What did you change in the live task, and how did you check it? Expected: A concrete change and a check against a known value.
- What will you learn next? Expected: A specific skill with a first step.

## Online version

For a hybrid group. The live class is 90 minutes on video with screen sharing. The learner finishes the build in their own time, about an hour. The full rules are in handbook/HYBRID_DELIVERY.md.

The showcase is best held in person. Use this clock only when a family cannot come. No recording without written consent.

### Live clock, 90 minutes

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 15 | Tech check, screen share test and the backup PDF ready on each laptop |
| 5 | Welcome for families on the video call |
| 45 | Presentations and questions: about nine minutes for each learner, five minutes of talk and the rest for questions |
| 15 | Feedback for every learner: one strength and one next step, then the next learning goal |
| 10 | Close: thanks, the project folder is sent by the route the organiser sets |

### After-class build task, about an hour

No build task. Send the project folder by the route the organiser sets, and keep a backup copy.

### Next class

There is no next class. Use the first minutes of any follow-up call to ask what each learner will learn next.

## Build log and feedback

Look at what each of the five learners saved and note one strength and one next step for each. Share them kindly and privately. Nothing is scored or ranked. Visit every learner, not only volunteers. Keep this guide private. Numeric results come from reference_values.json; explanations and show-and-tells need your judgement.
