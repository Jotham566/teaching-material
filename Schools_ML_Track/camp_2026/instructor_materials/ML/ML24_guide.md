# ML24 Instructor guide: Parent showcase and next steps

Private. Keep this file out of the student folder.

**Objective.** By the end: you can present your project, explain one decision, try one small change live with no AI assistant open, and name your next learning goal.

**Question of the day.** Can I show my project and explain how I built it?

**Where this is used.** Start-ups and tech hubs hold demo days where builders show what they made in a few minutes and answer questions. Today is your demo day. What do you want your family to remember?

Prerequisite: ML23 Explain the project to a parent.

## The 180 minutes

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 30 | final setup and screenshot backup check |
| 10 | welcome |
| 40 | presentations and questions |
| 15 | break |
| 25 | for one small live change by each learner |
| 20 | shared feedback and certificates |
| 40 | project folder, reflection and next-step plan |

Where it overruns: For five learners the presentations fit in 40 minutes. A group of 15 needs a separate event slot.

## Block A. Present and explain

Each learner has five minutes to present and three minutes for questions. Explain the input, the output and one failure case.

Ask for the prediction first: Write the one decision in your project you will explain if asked.

Correct result: Every learner follows the same friendly order. You show your own work, explain one decision, and answer a question from the audience about your code. Close assistant windows and turn off generative suggestions while you present. If the technology fails, use the screenshot backup and explain the steps; a short live rerun can be arranged later.

Investigate, with answers:

- What is a failure case? Answer: An input where the model does badly, with a reason.
- What if the laptop fails? Answer: Show the backup and explain the workflow. Rerun later if evidence is missing.

Modify: Say one thing your model gets wrong and why. Answer: For example: it misses fares for very long trips, because it learned from short ones.

## Block B. A small change, live

The instructor suggests one small change to your code, such as a different split size or one more input. Make it and say what you expect.

Ask for the prediction first: Before the change, predict whether your error will go up or down.

Correct result: The change is small on purpose. It is a chance to show how your notebook works. The programme certificate goes to learners who attended or made up most classes, kept their build log and showed a finished project. The camp has no tests and no scores. The certificate is issued by the programme provider and is not a university qualification or a certificate from another course provider.

Investigate, with answers:

- What does the certificate show? Answer: That you finished the track: you came to most classes, kept your build log and showed a project.
- What is a good next learning goal? Answer: One specific thing, such as a second model on the same data, or a chart you did not finish.

Modify: Write your next learning goal on one line. Answer: A specific goal you can start next week.

## Make: reference

The instructor gives one strength and one next step, kindly and privately, and notes that the learner finished the programme.

Build log entry to look for: A showcase talk, one small live change, one strength and one next step, and a certificate decision based on attendance, build log and finished project.

## Misconceptions to listen for

- The certificate is a university qualification.
- I need AI to change my code.
- A mistake while showing my project means I did badly.

## Questions to ask, with expected answers

- What do the instructor and the audience look for in the showcase? Expected: That you can explain your own work, through one decision and one small change.
- What if I missed some classes? Expected: Make them up with the instructor. The certificate needs most classes attended or made up.

## Online version

For a hybrid group. The live class is 90 minutes on video with screen sharing. The learner finishes the build in their own time, about an hour. The full rules are in handbook/HYBRID_DELIVERY.md.

The showcase is best held in person. Use this clock only when a family cannot come. No recording without written consent.

### Live clock, 90 minutes

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 15 | Tech check, screen share test and the backup PDF ready on each laptop |
| 5 | Welcome for families on the video call |
| 45 | Presentations and questions: about nine minutes for each learner, five minutes of talk and the rest for questions |
| 15 | Feedback for every learner: one strength and one next step, then the next learning goal |
| 10 | Close: thanks, the project folder is sent by the route the organiser sets |

### After-class build task, about an hour

No build task. Send the project folder by the route the organiser sets, and keep a backup copy.

### Next class

There is no next class. Use the first minutes of any follow-up call to ask what each learner will learn next.

## Build log and feedback

Look at what each of the five learners saved and note one strength and one next step for each. Share them kindly and privately. Nothing is scored or ranked. Visit every learner, not only volunteers.
