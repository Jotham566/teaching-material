# Machine Learning foundation labs

The revised proposed timetable adds four two-hour guided labs, one on a fourth teaching day in each of weeks 1–4. Weeks 5–8 return to three core classes. Data Analysis remains three classes weekly. Core class duration remains proposed at three hours including a break. ML: 24 core classes + 4 labs = 28 meetings, 80 scheduled hours including breaks; DA: 24 meetings, 72 scheduled hours including breaks. Confirm dated group timetables before enrolment.

Keep two 45–60 minute practice blocks on non-class days. Labs are guided practice, not extra homework. The sequence below is mandatory: each lab follows its week’s three core lessons and precedes the next week. Do not assign a lab before its stated prerequisite.

## Week 1: ML03 → F01 → next week's core lessons

Numbers, names and predicting Python output. Output: F01.ipynb with a worked result, individual variation and plain-language explanation.

## Week 2: ML06 → F02 → next week's core lessons

Lists as vectors and ordered measurements. Output: F02.ipynb with a worked result, individual variation and plain-language explanation.

## Week 3: ML09 → F03 → next week's core lessons

Variation, proportions and probability. Output: F03.ipynb with a worked result, individual variation and plain-language explanation.

## Week 4: ML12 → F04 → next week's core lessons

Matrices, weighted sums and a prediction rule. Output: F04.ipynb with a worked result, individual variation and plain-language explanation.

## Readiness before ML13

Each learner must explain a variable, index a list, read a row/column table, calculate a mean and proportion, and work through one weighted sum with units. Check individually using F01–F04. Use a repair example if a skill is missing; do not interpret attendance as understanding. Advanced calculus, matrix factorisation and deep learning remain later study.
