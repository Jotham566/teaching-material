## Words for today

| Word | What it means | Think of it like this |
|---|---|---|
| select | Name a column in square brackets to look only at it. | Reading only the price strip of the register. |
| filter | Keep only the rows where a comparison is True. | Picking out the sales of 5 units or more. |
| mask | A column of True and False values, one for each row, that a filter uses. | A ticked list beside the register. |
| new column | A column worked out from other columns and added to the table. | Multiplying units by price on every line. |
| revenue | Money received from sales: units times price. It is not profit. | The takings before paying for stock. |
| sum | All the values added together. | Adding up the day's takings. |

## Block A. Select and filter

Read the practice shop. Square brackets with a column name select one column. A comparison inside square brackets keeps only the rows where it is True.

**Predict.** Predict how many of the 60 sales have 5 units or more. Write your guess on paper first.

```python
import pandas as pd
shop = pd.read_csv("camp_shop.csv")
units = shop["units"]
big = shop[shop["units"] >= 5]
print(len(big))
print(units.sum())
```

**Run it, then compare.** The cell prints 30 and then 277. shop["units"] selects one column. The comparison gives True or False for every row, and only the True rows stay. All 60 sales together sold 277 units.

**Investigate.**

1. Is the filter deleting the other 30 rows?
2. Why can the sum of units not tell us the money earned?

**Modify.** Filter the Juice sales with 5 units or more and count them.

## Block B. A new revenue column

Assigning to a new column name creates the column. Multiplying two columns multiplies their matching rows. This is how units times price becomes revenue.

**Predict.** Predict the total revenue, using the item prices 3000, 1000 and 2000. Write your guess on paper first.

```python
shop["revenue_ugx"] = shop["units"] * shop["unit_price_ugx"]
print(shop.head(3))
print(shop["revenue_ugx"].sum())
```

**Run it, then compare.** The first three revenues are 3000, 2000 and 8000. The total revenue is 550000 shillings. This is money taken in. There is no cost column, so profit cannot be worked out.

**Investigate.**

1. Why is revenue not profit?
2. Does the new column change camp_shop.csv?

**Modify.** Find the revenue of the Book sales only.
