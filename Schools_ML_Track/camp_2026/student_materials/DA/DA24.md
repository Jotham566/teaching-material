## Make

Present your project for five minutes and answer three minutes of questions. Explain one calculation, one chart and one limitation. The instructor then chooses one small change, and you make it unaided. Close assistant windows and turn off generative suggestions first. Acknowledge any help you had. Choose one next learning goal. Keep your project folder and feedback.

Save your work as DA24. Keep the worked example and put your own attempt below it. Write two sentences: what you changed and how you checked it.

## Error card

| You see | It means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The notebook will not open on the day | The file or environment was not tested on the showcase laptop. | Use the PDF and screenshots, explain the workflow, and arrange a short live rerun afterwards. |
| A question about the code cannot be answered | The student has not traced that line since they wrote it. | Say what the line is meant to do, then run it on a small input to show it. |
