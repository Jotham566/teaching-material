# DA21 Build and test the chart report

Week 7, Show it | Session 21 of 24 | Python for Data Analysis

**Today's question.** Does my report answer my questions without a verbal tour?

**Objective.** By the end: you can build a chart report that a stranger reads in 30 seconds, and you can critique a coding assistant's hint with a test.

Before this session: DA20 Build a reliable analysis. If you cannot explain the last example, use its error card with the instructor first.

## Words for today

| Word | What it means | Think of it like this |
|---|---|---|
| chart report | A short notebook of charts, numbers and sentences that answers the project questions. | A news page with pictures and captions. |
| caption | One sentence under a chart that says what it shows. | The words under a photograph. |
| data source note | A line that says where the data came from and its limits. | The credit under a map. |

## Make

Build the chart report. Use few charts, readable labels with units, the covered dates and a visible data source note. Write two findings and one limitation next to their evidence. Give a peer 30 seconds to find the largest category and its total, and record where they hesitate. To put a chart in your report, call plt.savefig("revenue.png", bbox_inches="tight") before plt.show(), then insert the PNG with Insert and Pictures. When the instructor confirms you are ready, spend 20 minutes with AI_CODING_ASSISTANTS_LAB.md: critique a hint on the practice function, run a test and log it in AI_USE_LOG.md.

Save your work as DA21. Keep the worked example and put your own attempt below it. Write two sentences: what you changed and how you checked it.

## Error card

| You see | It means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The saved picture cuts off the labels | The figure was saved without room for the labels. | Add bbox_inches="tight" to plt.savefig. |
| The picture is saved blank | plt.show() ran before plt.savefig, which clears the figure. | Call plt.savefig before plt.show. |
