## Error card

| You see | It means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The category totals add to 6375900, not 6250500 | The clean table was not used, or the excluded rows were put back by a merge. | Total from the cleaned table and compare with len() at each step. |
| MergeError or a larger row count after the merge | The key is repeated in the products table. | Check is_unique on the lookup table before you merge. |
