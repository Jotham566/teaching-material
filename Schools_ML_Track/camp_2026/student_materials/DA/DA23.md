# DA23 Explain it to a parent

Week 8, Hand over | Session 23 of 24 | Python for Data Analysis

**Today's question.** Can a parent say what I found, how I know, and what the data cannot tell us?

**Objective.** By the end: you can explain your project to a listener who has never seen code, in five minutes, with a chart and a limit.

Before this session: DA22 Report and handover. If you cannot explain the last example, use its error card with the instructor first.

## Make

Make five slides: my question; what data I used; what I did; my most useful finding with its chart; the limitation and next step. Save as showcase.pptx and keep a PDF and screenshots as a backup. Rehearse with a timer. Practise answering: where did this number come from, what did you clean, and what can this data not tell us. Disclose any help or AI use. Show only invented or approved non-personal data. The instructor may follow the rehearsal with MODERN_AI_ORIENTATION.md, a 20-minute optional introduction with no assignment or mark.

Save your work as DA23. Keep the worked example and put your own attempt below it. Write two sentences: what you changed and how you checked it.

## Error card

| You see | It means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| The talk runs over five minutes | There are too many slides or too much code on screen. | Keep one idea per slide and cut the slide that explains the method in detail. |
| The projector fails | The demonstration depends on a live notebook or the internet. | Use the PDF and the screenshots, and explain the workflow aloud. |
