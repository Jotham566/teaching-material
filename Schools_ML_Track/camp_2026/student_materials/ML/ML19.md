## Learning objective

By the end of this class: Write a narrow project plan with source, target, available inputs, baseline and evaluation method.

## Understand the idea

A project plan fixes a question, dataset, target, inputs, baseline, evaluation method and limits. Use synthetic_homes.csv for regression or bundled iris for classification. Pick the route you can explain. Each learner produces individual evidence, even when discussing ideas with the group. Real personal or school records are outside this beginner project. Review AI_USE_POLICY.md before project work: limited supervised assistance may be demonstrated only after an independent attempt; record any permitted use in AI_USE_LOG.md.

## Follow the worked example

1. Read the example before running or typing it. Say what you expect to happen.
2. The instructor demonstrates it, explaining each new term. Follow one step at a time.
3. Compare your output with the expected result below. If it differs, use the repair cue.

Example: predict synthetic rent from area and rooms. Target: rent_ugx. Inputs available before prediction: area_m2 and rooms. Compare with training mean. Measure MAE in UGX. State explicitly that the data is invented for teaching.

Expected result: A saved plan and a column dictionary with units or categories.

## Practise between classes

Spend 20 to 30 minutes repeating the worked example without copying, then change one input and predict the new result. Stop after 30 minutes if stuck, save the error or a screenshot, and bring the question to the next class. On project weeks use the same time to complete the stated milestone. Plan two 45 to 60 minute practice blocks on non-class days; leave time for rest and school work.
