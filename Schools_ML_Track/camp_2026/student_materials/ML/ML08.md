## Words for today

| Word | What it means | Think of it like this |
|---|---|---|
| select a column | Name the column in square brackets to look only at it. | Reading only the price line of the register. |
| filter | Keep only the rows where a comparison is True. | Picking out the sales of 5 units or more. |
| new column | A column calculated from other columns, added to the table. | Multiplying units by price on every line. |
| sum | All the values added together. | Adding up the day's takings. |
| group | Gather rows with the same label before calculating. | Sorting the sales into three piles by item. |
| revenue | Money received from sales: units times price. It is not profit. | The takings before paying for stock. |

## Block A. Select and filter

Read the practice shop and keep the large sales.

**Predict.** Predict how many of the 60 sales have 5 units or more. Write your guess on paper first.

```python
import pandas as pd
shop = pd.read_csv("camp_shop.csv")
units = shop["units"]
big = shop[shop["units"] >= 5]
print(len(big))
print(units.sum())
```

**Run it, then compare.** The cell prints 30 and 277. shop["units"] selects one column. The comparison inside the outer brackets gives True or False for every row, and only the True rows stay. That filter keeps 30 sales. All 60 sales together sold 277 units.

**Investigate.**

1. Is the filter deleting the other 30 rows?
2. Why can the sum of units not tell us the money earned?

## Block B. A revenue column and totals by item

Multiply units by price on every row, then add the money for each item.

**Predict.** Predict which item earns the most money. Is it the one with the most units? Write your guess on paper first.

```python
shop["revenue_ugx"] = shop["units"] * shop["unit_price_ugx"]
total_revenue = shop["revenue_ugx"].sum()
by_item = shop.groupby("item")["revenue_ugx"].sum()
print(total_revenue)
print(by_item)
```

**Run it, then compare.** The total is 550000 shillings. Book earns 297000, Juice 150000 and Pen 103000. Pen sold the most units, 103, but Book earns the most money because each Book costs 3000. Assigning to a new column name creates the column, and the groupby line adds the revenue of each item separately.

**Investigate.**

1. Pen sold the most units. Why is it last for revenue?
2. Do the three item totals add up to the overall total?

**Modify.** Group the units column by item instead and print the totals.
