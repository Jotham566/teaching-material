## Words for today

| Word | What it means | Think of it like this |
|---|---|---|
| value | One piece of information, such as 2000 or Juice. | One entry in a receipt. |
| integer | A whole number with no decimal part. | Counting 3 books. |
| float | A number with a decimal part. | A price of 2500.5 shillings. |
| string | Text inside quotation marks. | A name written on a label. |
| variable | A name that points to a value. | A label on a box of stock. |
| type | The kind of value: integer, float or string. | Knowing whether a box holds pens or juice. |

## Block A. Names point to values

A shop sells 3 items at UGX 2000 each. Run the cell and read each line of output.

**Predict.** Predict the three lines the cell will print. Write your guess on paper first.

```python
price = 2000
quantity = 3
cost = price * quantity
print(cost)
print(type(price))
print(type("2000"))
```

**Run it, then compare.** The cell prints 6000, then <class 'int'>, then <class 'str'>. price and quantity are variables. 2000 is an integer. The quoted 2000 is a string, which is text. The equals sign stores the value on its right under the name on its left.

**Investigate.**

1. Why is type("2000") a string?
2. What does the equals sign do in cost = price * quantity?

**Modify.** Change quantity to 5 and run the whole cell again.

## Block B. A name keeps its value until you change it

Run the cell and watch what the printed total does when quantity changes.

**Predict.** Predict the three numbers printed. Will the second one change after quantity changes? Write your guess on paper first.

```python
price = 2500.5
quantity = 2
total = price * quantity
print(total)
quantity = 4
print(total)
total = price * quantity
print(total)
```

**Run it, then compare.** The cell prints 5001.0, then 5001.0, then 10002.0. 2500.5 is a float. Changing quantity does not change total until the line that calculates total runs again. Python works from the top of the cell to the bottom.

**Investigate.**

1. Why does the second print still show 5001.0?
2. What line makes the third number different?

**Modify.** Set quantity to 10, recalculate total and print it.
