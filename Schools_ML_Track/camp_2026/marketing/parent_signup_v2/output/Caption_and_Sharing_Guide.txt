This holiday, give your child the opportunity to build their first data project and explain what they have learned.

The December 2026 Holiday Data & AI Camp is for S.4, S.5 and S.6 students and school leavers. No coding experience needed.

Data Analysis learners will work with datasets, create charts and present a portfolio project. Machine Learning learners will build and test a prediction model.

With a maximum of five learners per group, the programme offers guided practice over eight weeks.

Tutor: Jotham Martin Wambi, Lecturer in AI & Machine Learning at IUEA and Data Scientist & ML/AI Engineer. Master's from Yamaguchi University, Japan.

Programme fee: UGX 1,000,000 per learner.
Programme capacity: 15 learners in total. Three learning days each week: two in person at The National ICT Innovation Hub, Nakawa, and one online. Other days are for project work. A Windows or Mac laptop is required. Start date and session times are to be confirmed. Please enquire about current availability.
