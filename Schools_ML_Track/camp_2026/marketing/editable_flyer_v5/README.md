## Editing

Duplicate the PowerPoint and use its Selection Pane to select named objects. Text, panels and divider lines are native objects. Photos, logos, ticks and outcome icons are independent images. In the source assets, the internet icons are also available as SVGs.

Use Arial, Arial Black, Arial Narrow and Caveat. Install the included Caveat.ttf before editing on another computer. PDF fonts are embedded. Keep the portrait’s aspect ratio locked, and use Fit/Contain rather than stretching or cropping. Keep the QR square and retain its white border.

After editing the PowerPoint, export new PNG/PDF files. Existing exported files do not update automatically. For source-based changes, edit `source/layout.json` and use the supplied exporters with the Codex artifact runtime and ReportLab. `build_layout.py` depends on the preceding local edition and is included as an assembly record; the standalone exporters use the included JSON and assets.
