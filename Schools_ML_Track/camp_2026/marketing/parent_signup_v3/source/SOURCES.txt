The example trains nearest-neighbour classifiers with different neighbour-weighting settings on two Iris measurements: sepal length and sepal width. It displays the original observations and prediction regions. It should not be described as an image-recognition model or as a camp learner's project.
Dataset: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/53/iris
