PYTHON FOR DATA ANALYSIS
Turn raw data into clear findings. Learn to clean data, compare groups, explore patterns with charts, merge tables and turn evidence into findings.
Tools: Python, Jupyter, NumPy, pandas, matplotlib and seaborn.
Your individual project: a Python analysis notebook, charts, a cleaning log and a short report. Example: identify the best-selling products in sample sales data and explain the evidence.
24 classes: three per week; other days for project practice.

MACHINE LEARNING
Build prediction models and learn how to test whether they work. Learn Python, explore data, compare models with a baseline and explain prediction errors.
Tools: Python, Jupyter, NumPy, pandas and scikit-learn.
Your individual project: a Python model notebook, baseline comparison and test results. Example: estimate rent from sample housing data.
24 classes plus four foundation labs: three classes weekly, with an extra lab in each of weeks 1–4. Other days for practice.

AI LITERACY
Near the end of the programme, learn how AI coding assistants can help with coding and debugging—and why their answers must be checked. Foundations and core exercises are completed without AI-generated solutions.

WHAT STUDENTS LEAVE WITH
Practical skills, an individual project they can explain, presentation experience at a parent/guardian showcase, and a completion certificate after meeting assessment and project requirements. Projects use synthetic sample data. This is a beginner programme, not a professional qualification.

Build a foundation for university study in computing, engineering, business, economics, statistics and data science.
