# School Track — restored original design

This version combines the original-style classroom background, large photographic portrait, brush accents, navy biography/booking cards and books-and-mug footer with all the newly requested course topics and differentiated Build outcomes.

The middle is 240 pixels taller to fit the additional copy. The top and footer retain their original proportions and placement relative to their own sections. Canvas: 1024 × 1776; sharing PNG: 2048 × 3552.

## Files

- `output/School_Track_Restored_Editable.pptx`: editable PowerPoint master.
- `output/School_Track_Restored.png`: sharing image.
- `output/School_Track_Restored.pdf`: A4 portrait with side margins, selectable text and contact links; no print bleed.
- `output/School_Track_Restored.svg`: layered editable document.
- `source/layout.json`: editable text, coordinates and styling.
- `assets/`: actual original portrait, transparent foreground cutout, background artwork, decorative accents, QR, logos, fonts and licenses.

## Your actual photo

The face and clothing come from your supplied JPEG. Only its grey background was masked out to allow the classroom to show behind it. The portrait is an independent image object and was not generated by ImageGen. The unmodified JPEG remains included. The environmental background and brushwork are reconstructed image assets based on the original flyer; those individual photographic objects are not editable vector artwork.

## Editability

Marketing text, course topics, Build outcomes, tutor biography, book/mug lettering, fees and captions are native text in PowerPoint. Panels/dividers are native shapes. Portrait, background, brush layers, logos and QR are separate image objects. Use PowerPoint's Selection Pane to choose items by name. Keep the QR square and preserve its white border.

Fonts: Arial, Arial Black, Arial Narrow and Caveat. Caveat is installed on the current Mac and included in `assets/Caveat.ttf`. Install it before editing on another computer to avoid substitutions. PDF fonts are embedded.

After editing, export fresh PNG/PDF files; the supplied exports do not update themselves. Source-based changes can be made in layout.json and rebuilt with export_powerpoint.mjs (Codex artifact runtime) and export_print.py (ReportLab with macOS font paths). build_layout.py assembles the layout from prior local editions and is kept for provenance; the standalone exporters need only the included JSON/assets. Close and reopen PowerPoint if a newly installed font is not visible.

Sources and photographic handling are documented in `source/SOURCES.md`; the background prompt is in `source/IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`.
