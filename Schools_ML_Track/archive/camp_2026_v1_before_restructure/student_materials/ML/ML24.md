Complete ML23 Explain the project to a parent. If you cannot explain the previous example, use its repair cue with the instructor before moving on.

## Learning objective

By the end of this class: Demonstrate, explain and modify a small part of the project without AI assistance.

## Understand the idea

Demonstrating learning means showing your own work and explaining a decision. The certificate records the completed track and assessed evidence under the published completion policy. It is issued by the programme provider; it is not a university qualification or an external vendor certification.

## Follow the worked example

1. Read the example before running or typing it. Say what you expect to happen.
2. The instructor demonstrates it, explaining each new term. Follow one step at a time.
3. Compare your output with the expected result below. If it differs, use the repair cue.

Each student has five minutes to demonstrate and three minutes for questions. Explain the input, output and one failure case. Give the parent a local portfolio copy or an approved private link.

Expected result: An individual demonstration, feedback and a recorded completion decision.

## Your turn

Present your project, acknowledge help, and choose one next learning goal. Keep your project folder and feedback after camp. Demonstrate and explain your work without AI assistance. Close assistant windows and disable generative suggestions; answer an instructor-chosen code question unaided.

Save your work using the session code ML24. Keep the original worked example and put your own attempt below it or in a separate sheet/file. Add two sentences: what I changed and how I checked it. Follow AI_USE_POLICY.md: weeks 1–6 permit no AI-generated solutions for core exercises, labs or assessments. Final assessment must be demonstrated and explained without AI assistance.

## When something goes wrong

If technology fails, use the screenshot backup and explain the workflow; arrange a short live rerun later if required evidence is missing.

## Practise between classes

Spend 20 to 30 minutes repeating the worked example without copying, then change one input and predict the new result. Stop after 30 minutes if stuck, save the error or a screenshot, and bring the question to the next class. On project weeks use the same time to complete the stated milestone. Plan two 45 to 60 minute practice blocks on non-class days; leave time for rest and school work.

## Check before leaving

Show your saved file, explain one new term, and point to the evidence that your answer works. Use the submission instructions in START_HERE.md for any assessed work. A file saved on your laptop has not automatically been submitted.
