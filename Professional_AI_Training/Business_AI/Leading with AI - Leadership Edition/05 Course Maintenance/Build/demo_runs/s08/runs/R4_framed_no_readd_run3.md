**Results: net pay UGX 1,802,898; total cost to company UGX 2,261,250.**

**Working (unrounded values carried through, rounded only at the end)**
1. Hourly rate = 1,800,000 / 160 = 11,250.
2. Weekday overtime = 12 x 11,250 x 1.5 = 12 x 16,875 = 202,500.
3. Sunday work = 6 x 11,250 x 2 = 6 x 22,500 = 135,000.
4. Unpaid leave deduction = 1 x 1,800,000 / 22 = 81,818.18 (81,818.1818...).
5. Gross pay = 1,800,000 + 202,500 + 135,000 - 81,818.18 = 2,055,681.82.
6. Employee pension (5%) = 0.05 x 2,055,681.82 = 102,784.09.
7. Advance repayment = 150,000.
8. **Net pay** = 2,055,681.82 - 102,784.09 - 150,000 = 1,802,897.73, which rounds to **UGX 1,802,898**.
9. Employer pension (10%) = 0.10 x 2,055,681.82 = 205,568.18.
10. **Total cost to company** = 2,055,681.82 + 205,568.18 = 2,261,250.00, which is **UGX 2,261,250**.

**Assumptions on unclear rules**
- The rules don't say whether pension is also taken on the overtime pay. I followed the rule as written: it applies to gross pay, which includes overtime and is net of the leave deduction.
- The advance is taken after the pension contribution, as stated. It is not part of the company's cost because it is a repayment of money already advanced.
- The rules don't say when to round. I kept full precision through the calculation and rounded only the two final figures. If you round each line to the nearest shilling first, the net pay comes to 1,802,898 and the total cost to 2,261,250, so the answers are the same.
- The 160-hour basis for the hourly rate and the 22-day basis for the leave deduction are different. I used each as the rules state, without reconciling them.
