**Results**
- Net pay: UGX 1,802,898
- Total cost to the company: UGX 2,261,250

**Working** (full precision kept, rounded only at the end)
1. Hourly rate = 1,800,000 / 160 = 11,250
2. Weekday overtime = 12 h x 1.5 x 11,250 = 202,500
3. Sunday work = 6 h x 2 x 11,250 = 135,000
4. Unpaid leave deduction = 1,800,000 / 22 x 1 day = 81,818.18
5. Gross pay = 1,800,000 + 202,500 + 135,000 - 81,818.18 = 2,055,681.82 (UGX 2,055,682)
6. Employee pension (5%) = 102,784.09
7. Advance repayment = 150,000
8. Net pay = 2,055,681.82 - 102,784.09 - 150,000 = 1,802,897.73, which rounds to 1,802,898
9. Employer pension (10%) = 205,568.18
10. Total cost = 2,055,681.82 + 205,568.18 = 2,261,250.00

**Check by re-adding the rounded lines**
- Gross 2,055,682 - 102,784 - 150,000 = 1,802,898, which matches the net pay.
- Gross 2,055,682 + 205,568 = 2,261,250, which matches the total cost.
- Net pay 1,802,898 + employee pension 102,784 + advance 150,000 + employer pension 205,568 = 2,261,250, so every shilling is accounted for.

**Assumptions where a rule was unclear**
- **Sunday and overtime hours:** I treated the 12 overtime hours and 6 Sunday hours as extra to the 160 contracted hours, so they are not already in the salary.
- **Pension base:** Pension is charged on the full gross pay, including overtime, after the leave deduction, as the rules state.
- **Taxes:** No PAYE, NSSF or other statutory deductions are in the rules, so I included none. A real Ugandan payslip would normally have them.
- **Advance and total cost:** The advance is a repayment of money already paid out, so it lowers net pay but does not change the company's cost.
- **Rounding:** The leave deduction is 81,818.18, not a whole number of shillings. I kept the decimals through the calculation and rounded only the final figures. Rounding each line first gives the same answers.
