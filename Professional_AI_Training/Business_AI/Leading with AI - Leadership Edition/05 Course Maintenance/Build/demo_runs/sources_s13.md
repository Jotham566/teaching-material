## Uganda, Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 (read in full text)
- Source: ULII, Chapter 97, "Legislation as at 3 May 2019" (https://ulii.org/akn/ug/act/2019/9/eng@2019-05-03; PDF https://media.ulii.org/media/legislation/18002/source_file/98f959926ba2429c/2019-9.pdf). The PDF was read on 6 November 2026.
- Section 1: applies to collecting, processing, holding or using personal data within Uganda, and to those outside Uganda who handle personal data of Ugandan citizens.
- Section 2: "personal data" is information about a person from which the person can be identified, recorded in any form.
- Section 19: a Ugandan controller or processor that processes or stores data outside Uganda must ensure the other country has protection at least equivalent to the Act, or that the data subject consented.
- Sections 20 and 21: reasonable technical and organisational security measures; a contract with a processor must require them.
- Section 23: on belief that personal data was accessed or acquired by an unauthorised person, notify the Authority "immediately"; the Authority decides whether the data subject is told.
- Section 29: the Authority keeps a data protection register. Commentary (Cliffe Dekker Hofmeyr, Aug 2025; CIPESA, Jul 2025) says the PDPO ruled in July 2025 that Google must register; commentary on whether and how every company must register conflicts, so the slide notes leave it to the lawyer.
- Not on slides: the 2021 Regulations (not read in full text).

## EU AI Act
- Article 2 scope read at https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/2/ : covers providers placing systems on the EU market whether or not established in the EU, and providers and deployers in third countries when the output of the system is used in the Union.
- 2026 revisions: Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 per Cloud Security Alliance, Sidley, CMS and DLA Piper notes (search, 6 November 2026): Annex III high-risk date moved from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027, Annex I to 2 August 2028.
