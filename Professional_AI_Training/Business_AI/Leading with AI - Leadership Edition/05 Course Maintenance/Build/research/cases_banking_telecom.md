# AI cases: banking and microfinance, telecom and mobile money

Research date: 11 November 2026. All sources were accessed on 11 November 2026. Figures are quoted only from pages that were opened and read. Where a page blocked automated reading, it is not cited as a source for figures.

## Summary

This file holds 26 cases: 14 in banking and microfinance (BNK) and 12 in telecom and mobile money (TEL). By geography: 11 are East African (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania), 7 are from the rest of Africa (South Africa, Nigeria, Ghana, pan-African operators), and 8 are global benchmarks (Australia, Singapore, Sweden, US, UK). The strongest East African material is about digital credit scored from mobile-money data (M-Shwari, Fuliza, KCB, Airtel/GnuGrid, JUMO), fraud and spam detection (Safaricom, Airtel), and the governance backlash (Kenya data-protection fines, the 2026 High Court petitions against Safaricom's Zuri chatbot and credit algorithms, and Bank of Uganda's AI governance framework). There are 8 failure, regulator or mixed cases: BNK-05, BNK-06, BNK-09, BNK-10 (mixed), BNK-13, TEL-02, TEL-04 and TEL-12. The people angle is strongest in Equity Group (mandatory GenAI training for over 62% of staff), Capitec (AI growth while headcount rose by 711), Commonwealth Bank of Australia (45 call-centre roles cut and then reinstated), Klarna (AI replaced agents, then humans were rehired), DBS (4,000 contract roles to go) and BT (about 10,000 roles to AI). Most African results are company claims. Few have been independently audited, and teachers should say so.

# Part A: Banking and microfinance

### BNK-01 Equity Group: mandatory GenAI training for the whole workforce
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Equity Group Holdings, Kenya (East Africa), banking, 2025 to 2026
- **What the AI does:** Equity says it is building AI into customer engagement, risk management and operations, moving from an IT-led model to AI inside core functions. It made AI upskilling mandatory for staff in every role.
- **Result:** Over 7,300 employees trained and certified in generative AI through the Huawei ICT Academy, "over 62 percent" of an 11,900-strong workforce. 53 staff enrolled in a financial engineering master's at WorldQuant University. The plan is to scale advanced AI training to up to 5,000 staff "over time". 98.2% of transactions happen outside branches (period not stated). No published business result from AI.
- **Themes:** S5, S8, PEOPLE. **People angle:** all roles. CEO James Mwangi said staff cannot be placed in an AI-driven organisation without the skills to work in it. No job cuts were reported.
- **Source:** "Equity Group Bets on AI to Power Growth as Digital Transactions Hit 98.2%", TechTrends Kenya, https://techtrendske.co.ke/2026/03/25/equity-group-bets-on-ai-to-power-growth-as-digital-transactions-hit-98-2, 25 Mar 2026.
- **Confidence:** Medium (one credible secondary source reporting an investor briefing).
- **Caution:** These are training counts, not productivity results. Certification by a vendor academy (Huawei) is not the same as measured skill. Check Equity's FY2025 integrated report before quoting.

### BNK-02 KCB Group: data-driven scoring behind mobile lending
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** KCB Group, Kenya, banking, 2025
- **What the AI does:** KCB uses credit-scoring and behavioural-scoring models to decide who gets a mobile loan and how much, and reviews limits every two months. It added PesaLink transaction data and Hustler Fund repayment data to widen the pool of pre-scored customers.
- **Result (from KCB's integrated report as reported):** Mobile lending of Sh544 billion in 2025, up 30% year on year. Average disbursement of Sh1.5 billion a day. Credit eligibility up 7% and overall limits up 17% year on year from "enhanced scoring frameworks". Nearly 8 in 10 Kenyan loans are disbursed digitally (CBK figure, period not given). No default or NPL figure was published in the article.
- **Themes:** S2, S12, S14. **People angle:** none reported.
- **Source:** "KCB rides on data to disburse digital loans worth Sh1.5 billion daily", The Star (Kenya), https://www.the-star.co.ke/business/2026-05-07-kcb-rides-on-data-to-disburse-digital-loans-worth-sh15-billion-daily, 7 May 2026.
- **Confidence:** Medium.
- **Caution:** The article says "data analytics" and "scoring models", not "AI". Present it as prediction from data (S2). There is no figure on loan quality, so growth in lending is not evidence that the scoring is accurate.

### BNK-03 Numida: credit scoring for Ugandan micro-businesses without scraping phones
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Numida, Uganda (also Kenya), digital MSME lender, 2021 to 2022
- **What the AI does:** Numida scores small businesses with its own model, built from its loan history and business profiles (sector, cash flow, repayment behaviour), using only what the customer enters in the app. Repeat clients in good standing are approved instantly. New or larger requests can take up to 24 hours.
- **Result:** $20 million of working capital lent to micro and small businesses to date (Sept 2022). Monthly lending grew from $250,000 to $2 million. Loans of $100 to $5,000, due after one month, at 10% to 16% interest (average about 11.5%). No repayment or default rates were published.
- **Themes:** S2, S13. **People angle:** none reported.
- **Source:** "YC-backed fintech Numida raises $12.3M led by Serena Ventures to extend loans to MSMEs beyond Uganda", TechCrunch, https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/28/yc-backed-fintech-numida-raises-12-3m-led-by-serena-ventures-to-extend-loans-to-msmes-beyond-uganda, 28 Sep 2022.
- **Confidence:** Medium.
- **Caution:** The figures date from 2022 fundraising coverage. Its current status was not checked. A useful teaching contrast with BNK-05 and BNK-06: the CEO said Numida does not scrape phone contacts or social media.

### BNK-04 Bank of Uganda: building an AI governance framework
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Bank of Uganda (central bank), Uganda, 2025
- **What the AI does:** The governor said the Bank is developing a "Comprehensive AI Governance Framework" requiring fairness, transparency, accountability, validation and central human oversight. Its Research and Policy Directorate already uses machine-learning models to refine GDP and inflation forecasts.
- **Result:** No published result. Initiatives named: a Financial Inclusion Data Dashboard with gender-disaggregated insights, and staff training in Power BI, Python, R and Azure through communities of practice and partners (IMF, FSD Uganda, universities). The governor warned of herding, dependence on a few technology firms, and algorithmic bias.
- **Themes:** S2, S13, S14, PEOPLE (staff skills at the regulator).
- **Source:** Michael Atingi-Ego, Governor, Bank of Uganda, address to the 2025 Data Analytics, AI and Data Governance Symposium, Kampala, 11 Nov 2025, published by the Bank for International Settlements, https://www.bis.org/review/r251125a.pdf, 25 Nov 2025.
- **Confidence:** High (primary: the regulator's own speech).
- **Caution:** The framework was in development when he spoke. Check whether it has been issued before saying it is in force. Global statistics in the speech (for example, a 20% to 30% cut in false-positive reports) are general, not Bank of Uganda results.

### BNK-05 Mulla Pride (KeCredit, Faircash): Kenya data regulator fines a digital lender (FAILURE)
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Mulla Pride Ltd, Kenya, digital lending, 2023
- **What happened:** The lender got borrowers' contacts' names and details from third parties and used them to send intimidating messages and make threatening calls to recover debts. Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) fined it.
- **Result:** Fine of Sh2,975,000 (about $20,000 per TechCabal headline coverage), 26 Sep 2023.
- **Themes:** S10, S13. **People angle:** debt-collection practices; harm to borrowers' families and contacts.
- **Source:** "Mobile lender fined millions for breach of borrowers' privacy", The Star (Kenya), https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2023-09-26-mobile-lender-fined-millions-for-breach-of-borrowers-privacy, 26 Sep 2023. Corroborating headline: "Digital lenders fined in Kenya", TechCabal, 26 Sep 2023 (page blocked to automated reading, so not used for figures).
- **Confidence:** High (regulator action reported by two outlets; figure read from The Star).
- **Caution:** This is a data-protection and debt-collection failure, not a model failure. Use it to show that data-hungry lending apps create legal risk regardless of how good the score is. Context: CBK licensed 110 new digital credit providers in 2025 (85 to 195), and their outstanding loans rose 99.6% from Ksh55.2 billion (Dec 2024) to Ksh110.1 billion (Dec 2025), per CBK's Bank Supervision Annual Report as reported by The Kenya Times, https://thekenyatimes.com/business/fuliza-style-lending-explodes-as-digital-loans-hit-ksh110-billion/, 22 Sep 2026 (Medium confidence).

### BNK-06 Soko Lending (SokoLoan): Nigeria's data regulator fines a loan app (FAILURE)
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Soko Lending Company Ltd, Nigeria, digital lending, 2021
- **What happened:** The loan app required access to borrowers' phone contacts. When a borrower missed a repayment, it allegedly sent privacy-invading messages to the borrower's contacts.
- **Result:** NITDA fine of ₦10 million (Aug 2021). Other sanctions: no more such messages until it complies with the NDPR, a paid Data Protection Impact Assessment, nine months of mandatory oversight, and referral of criminal aspects to the Nigeria Police. Findings included sharing data without a lawful basis and embedding trackers that sent data to third parties without telling users.
- **Themes:** S10, S13.
- **Source:** "NITDA imposes N10 million fine on Soko Loan for data privacy invasion", ICIR Nigeria, https://www.icirnigeria.org/nitda-imposes-n10-million-fine-on-soko-loan-for-data-privacy-invasion/, 17 Aug 2021. Also reported by TechCabal, 24 Aug 2021 (not read).
- **Confidence:** Medium (one outlet read in full; the regulator's statement is quoted in it).
- **Caution:** The regulator was NITDA, not the FCCPC or today's NDPC. The case is from 2021.

### BNK-07 Capitec: AI fraud blocking while headcount still grows
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Capitec Bank, South Africa, retail banking, financial year reported April 2026
- **What the AI does:** AI scores payments and beneficiaries to block scams and mule accounts before money leaves. Separately, staff use AI tools daily, an in-house tool (Capitec Pulse AI) gives contact-centre agents real-time client information, and an AI agent sits inside business-banking credit processing.
- **Result:** In the year reported: more than 131,000 fraudulent beneficiaries blocked, more than 394,000 scam payments prevented, about R673 million in potential client losses avoided. About 5,000 employees have AI tools, averaging four interactions a day each. Total staff rose by 711, from 16,525 to 17,236 (+4.3%).
- **Themes:** S2, S3, S5, S8, S11, S12, PEOPLE. **People angle:** client-support agents get AI assistance. The bank says AI lets it scale "without proportional increases" in headcount, but staff numbers still rose.
- **Source:** "Capitec bets big on AI and keeps hiring", TechCentral, https://techcentral.co.za/capitec-bets-big-on-ai-and-keeps-hiring/280534/, 22 Apr 2026 (reporting Capitec's annual results).
- **Confidence:** Medium (one credible outlet read; the figures come from the bank's own report). Other outlets give R642m and R699m for nearby periods, so quote the period with the number.
- **Caution:** "Losses avoided" is the bank's own estimate. The number of legitimate payments wrongly blocked (false alarms) is not published, which makes this a good S3 discussion.

### BNK-08 Access, GTCO, UBA and Wema: fraud prevented, but AI's share is not isolated (MEASURE)
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Access Holdings, GTCO, UBA, Wema Bank, Nigeria, banking, 2025
- **What the AI does:** The banks use real-time fraud monitoring, device detection, liveness checks and authentication. Regulators are pushing them towards AI-based monitoring.
- **Result:** In 2025 the four banks faced about N17.5bn of attempted or involved fraud and blocked or recovered about N14.5bn. Access, GTCO and UBA lost N2.13bn of N10.29bn (20.66%). Wema blocked about 88% of attempts, but its losses rose to N847.6m from N29.45m in 2024. Access, GTCO and UBA together spent N280.90bn (about $206m) on technology in 2025.
- **Themes:** S2, S3, S12.
- **Source:** "How tech spend helps four banks block N14.5bn fraud losses in 2025", BusinessDay (Nigeria), https://businessday.ng/technology/article/how-tech-spend-helps-four-banks-block-n14-5bn-fraud-losses-in-2025/, 12 May 2026.
- **Confidence:** Medium.
- **Caution:** The article credits "detection and response systems" in general. It does not say how much is due to AI. Use it to teach executives to ask "what did the AI add, against what baseline?" Wema's rising losses show that a high block rate can sit beside rising losses.

### BNK-09 Commonwealth Bank of Australia: 45 call-centre jobs cut for a voice bot, then reinstated (FAILURE, PEOPLE)
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA), Australia, retail banking, July to August 2025
- **What happened:** CBA declared 45 customer-service roles redundant after bringing in an AI voice bot for simple inbound queries. The Finance Sector Union said call volumes were actually rising, with overtime offered and team leaders answering calls. CBA reversed the decision and apologised.
- **Result:** The bank said its initial assessment "did not adequately consider all relevant business considerations" and that "this error meant the roles were not redundant". The 45 staff could keep their role, move to another role, or take redundancy (per the union). No measured result for the bot was published in the sources read.
- **Themes:** S3, S5, S10, S12, PEOPLE. **People angle:** contact-centre staff, union pressure, reputational damage.
- **Sources:** "Bank reverses decision to replace 45 customer service staff with AI chatbot", The Register, https://www.theregister.com/2025/08/22/commonwealth_ban_chatbot_fail_rehiring/, 22 Aug 2025. "CBA reverses AI-driven job cuts, admits 'error'", Information Age (ACS), https://ia.acs.org.au/article/2025/cba-reverses-ai-driven-job-cuts--admits--error-.html, 21 Aug 2025.
- **Confidence:** High (two independent sources).
- **Caution:** Other reports say CBA claimed the bot cut calls by 2,000 a week. That figure was not on the pages read, so do not quote it. Some outlets call the tool a chatbot, others a voice bot.

### BNK-10 Klarna: AI assistant "did the work of 700 agents", then humans were rehired (MIXED, PEOPLE)
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Klarna, Sweden, payments and consumer credit (fintech), 2024 to 2025
- **What the AI does:** An OpenAI-powered assistant handled customer chats about refunds, returns and payments in more than 35 languages.
- **Result:** Klarna's February 2024 claim: the assistant handled 75% of customer chats, about 2.3 million conversations, in its first month, the work of 700 agents. In May 2025 the CEO said the AI output was cheaper but of "lower quality", and Klarna began rehiring human agents (remote roles) after a hiring freeze of more than a year.
- **Themes:** S3, S5, S10, S12, PEOPLE. **People angle:** customer-service agents and outsourced staff, hiring freeze, then rehiring.
- **Source:** "Klarna Is Hiring Customer Service Agents After AI Couldn't Cut It on Calls, According to the Company's CEO", Entrepreneur, https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/klarna-ceo-reverses-course-by-hiring-more-humans-not-ai/, 9 May 2025 (reporting a Bloomberg interview).
- **Confidence:** Medium (one outlet read; the original interview was in Bloomberg).
- **Caution:** The "700 agents" figure was a company claim and was never independently checked. Klarna is a fintech, not a deposit-taking East African bank. Use it as a global benchmark for counting chats handled rather than resolution quality.

### BNK-11 DBS Bank: 4,000 contract roles expected to go as AI takes over work (PEOPLE)
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** DBS Group, Singapore, banking, 2025 to 2028
- **What the AI does:** The CEO said AI would take over work currently done by people on project-based contract roles.
- **Result:** About 4,000 temporary and contract jobs to be cut over three years across 19 markets through natural attrition. Permanent staff were not affected. About 1,000 new AI-related jobs were expected (announced 24 Feb 2025).
- **Themes:** S12, PEOPLE. **People angle:** contract and temporary workers. The outgoing CEO said he was struggling to see how to repurpose people this time.
- **Source:** "DBS set to cut 4,000 temporary jobs over 3 years due to AI, CEO says", Reuters via Yahoo Finance, https://finance.yahoo.com/news/dbs-group-set-cut-4-095901808.html, 24 Feb 2025. Also reported by Bloomberg, 24 Feb 2025 (not read).
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Caution:** This is a forecast, not a completed outcome. Check progress before teaching.

### BNK-12 Morgan Stanley Wealth Management: an assistant grounded in the firm's own research
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Morgan Stanley, US, wealth management, 2023 to 2024
- **What the AI does:** "AI @ Morgan Stanley Assistant" (OpenAI-powered, fully rolled out September 2023) lets advisers ask questions of the firm's own intellectual capital. "Debrief" takes meeting notes with client consent, summarises them, drafts a follow-up email for the adviser to review, and saves a note to Salesforce.
- **Result:** 98% of financial-adviser teams had adopted the Assistant (June 2024, company figure). One adviser said Debrief saved him about half an hour per meeting (anecdote). No firm-wide productivity figure was published.
- **Themes:** S4, S5, S6, S8, S9, S13 (client consent), PEOPLE. **People angle:** financial advisers and their teams; the human reviews before anything is sent.
- **Source:** "Morgan Stanley Wealth Management Announces Latest Game Changing Addition to Suite of GenAI Tools", Morgan Stanley press release (Business Wire), republished by Silicon UK, https://www.silicon.co.uk/press-release/morgan-stanley-wealth-management-announces-latest-game-changing-addition-to-suite-of-genai-tools, 26 Jun 2024.
- **Confidence:** Medium (primary press release; adoption is self-reported).
- **Caution:** "Adopted" is not defined. Time saved is one adviser's anecdote.

### BNK-13 Apple Card and Goldman Sachs: the gender-bias investigation (REGULATOR)
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Goldman Sachs (issuer of Apple Card), US, consumer credit, 2019 to 2021
- **What happened:** After viral complaints in 2019 that the card's algorithm gave women lower credit limits, the New York Department of Financial Services (NYDFS) investigated the underwriting.
- **Result:** NYDFS analysed underwriting data for about 400,000 New York applicants and found no intentional discrimination and no disparate impact (23 Mar 2021). It criticised "deficiencies in customer service and a perceived lack of transparency" that undermined trust. Goldman and Apple improved transparency and dropped a rule that made approved applicants wait six months before appealing their terms. The Superintendent said credit-scoring models and anti-discrimination laws need updating.
- **Themes:** S2, S3, S10, S13.
- **Source:** "Goldman cleared of bias claims in NYDFS's Apple Card probe", Banking Dive, https://www.bankingdive.com/news/goldman-sachs-gender-bias-claims-apple-card-women-new-york-dfs/597273/, 24 Mar 2021.
- **Confidence:** Medium (one outlet read; it reports the regulator's own report).
- **Caution:** The algorithm was cleared. The lesson is that failing to explain a decision did the reputational damage, not proven bias.

### BNK-14 Bank of America: Erica for clients and for employees
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Bank of America, US, retail banking, 2018 to 2025
- **What the AI does:** Erica is a virtual assistant in the bank's app that answers client questions and sends proactive insights on spending and saving. "Erica for Employees" answers staff questions, including IT help.
- **Result (to Aug 2025):** Over 3 billion client interactions since 2018, nearly 50 million users, more than 58 million interactions a month on average. Over 98% of users say they find the information they need (company survey). Erica for Employees is used by over 90% of employees and cut calls to the IT service desk by 50%.
- **Themes:** S4, S5, S8, S12, PEOPLE. **People angle:** IT service-desk workload halved; near-universal staff use.
- **Source:** "A Decade of AI Innovation: BofA's Virtual Assistant Erica Surpasses 3 Billion Client Interactions", Bank of America press release (republished), https://www.placera.se/pressmeddelanden/a-decade-of-ai-innovation-bofa-s-virtual-assistant-erica-surpasses-3-billion-client-interactions-20250820, 20 Aug 2025.
- **Confidence:** Medium (primary company release; not independently checked).
- **Caution:** Company figures. Erica was built over seven years with scripted responses (over 700) as well as AI, so it is not an overnight GenAI rollout.

# Part B: Telecom and mobile money

### TEL-01 M-Shwari: lending decisions from phone and M-Pesa history
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Safaricom and Commercial Bank of Africa (now NCBA), Kenya, mobile money and digital credit, 2012 to 2015
- **What the AI does:** The bank scores applicants using their Safaricom phone and M-Pesa usage history to judge creditworthiness, set individual limits and lend to first-time applicants. Later versions also used customers' M-Shwari savings and repayment records.
- **Result (CGAP, April 2015):** Over 10 million accounts and 4.5 million active customers. 20.6 million loans worth $277 million to 2.8 million unique borrowers since November 2012, average loan $15. 50,000 loans disbursed per day. Non-performing loans (over 90 days): 2%. Fee: 7.5% per 30-day loan.
- **Themes:** S2, S3, S14. **People angle:** none; there are no loan officers in the process.
- **Source:** Tamara Cook and Claudia McKay, "Top 10 Things to Know About M-Shwari", CGAP, https://www.cgap.org/blog/top-10-things-know-about-m-shwari, 2 Apr 2015.
- **Confidence:** High (CGAP is a World Bank-housed research body with access to the partners' data).
- **Caution:** The figures are from 2015. Present it as the founding case, not today's numbers. Pair it with TEL-02 for the harms side.

### TEL-02 Fuliza and digital credit: late repayment, blacklisting and a forced reset (FAILURE / REGULATOR PRESSURE)
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Safaricom, NCBA and KCB (Fuliza), Kenya; digital lenders in Kenya and Tanzania; 2018 to 2022
- **What happened:** Algorithmically scored mobile credit spread fast. Surveys found many borrowers repaid late or defaulted, and millions of Kenyans were negatively listed at credit bureaus. In 2022 the President announced that 4 to 5 million Kenyans would be removed from credit-bureau blacklisting, and Fuliza cut fees.
- **Result:** CGAP and FSD Kenya phone surveys of over 7,000 mobile users: 47% of Kenyan and 56% of Tanzanian digital borrowers had repaid a loan late; 12% (Kenya) and 31% (Tanzania) had defaulted; 20% of Kenyan borrowers had cut food purchases to repay (CEGA, Dec 2018). Fuliza (Sept 2022): 28 million customers opted in. Ksh290 billion disbursed in six months to June 2022. Daily maintenance fees cut by about 40% for most brackets. 1.083% daily rate (395.2% annualised). Between four and five million Kenyans to be delisted from 1 Nov 2022.
- **Themes:** S2, S3, S10, S13.
- **Sources:** "Where are we now? 6 years after the first digital consumer credit product launched", CEGA (UC Berkeley), https://cega.berkeley.edu/article/where-are-we-now-6-years-after-the-first-digital-consumer-credit-product-launched/, Dec 2018. "4 million names to be delisted from CRB as Fuliza bows to pressure", Money254, https://money254.co.ke/post/4-million-names-to-be-delisted-from-crb-as-fuliza-bows-to-pressure-news, 28 Sep 2022.
- **Confidence:** Medium (CEGA is strong; the Fuliza figures come from one secondary outlet).
- **Caution:** The survey covers digital loans in general, not only Fuliza or M-Shwari. The delisting was a political announcement, not a court or regulator ruling. Present it as a cost of the speed of automated credit, not as proof the models were wrong.

### TEL-03 Safaricom: AI monitoring of M-Pesa finds laundering through betting firms
- **Organisation / country / industry / years:** Safaricom, Kenya, mobile money, FY2025
- **What the AI does:** Real-time transaction monitoring and machine-learning models flag unusual patterns across millions of M-Pesa transactions a day for anti-money-laundering, fraud and sanctions checks.
- **Result (Safaricom 2025 Sustainable Business Report, as reported):** Detected money laundering through betting platforms, flagged suspicious international transfers, found inflated agent deposits totalling 1.7 billion shillings, and reported payments linked to 14 wildlife poachers to the Financial Reporting Centre. Context: M-Pesa processes over 37 billion transactions a year worth 38.3 trillion shillings, with 35.8 million active users.
- **Themes:** S2, S3, S13, S14. **People angle:** M-Pesa agents (inflated deposits). Compliance teams act on alerts.
- **Sources:** "Kenya: Safaricom's AI uncovers money laundering schemes in betting firms on M-Pesa", iGamingToday, https://www.igamingtoday.com/kenya-safaricoms-ai-uncovers-money-laundering-schemes-in-betting-firms-on-m-pesa/, 21 Oct 2025.
