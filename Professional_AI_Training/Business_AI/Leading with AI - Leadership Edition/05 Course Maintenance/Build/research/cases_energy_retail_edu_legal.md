# Case research: energy and mining, retail/logistics/transport, education, legal and professional services

Researched 11 November 2026 against CASE_RESEARCH_BRIEF.md. All URLs accessed 11 Nov 2026.

## Summary

26 cases across four secondary industries: energy, mining, oil and gas (5), retail, logistics and transport (7), education (7), and legal and professional services (7). Every figure below comes from a page that was opened and read during this research. Where a page blocked automated reading, the figure was taken only if a second page that could be read confirmed it, and that is noted. Six cases are East African (Kenya Power, M-KOPA, Jumia (pan-African), Kenya Airways, EIDU, Kenya Judiciary). Three are from the rest of Africa (Edo State Nigeria, Rori in Ghana, Mavundla in South Africa). The other 17 are global benchmarks, so the mix leans global; the Gaps section explains why. Ten cases are failures or mixed results (Air Canada, DPD, McDonald's/IBM, Uber Eats, ACU, Chegg, Mata v Avianca, Mavundla, Ayinde/Al-Haroun, Deloitte Australia). Most of the success figures come from the company or its vendor. The strongest independent evidence is in education (randomised trials) and in law (court judgments).

## A. Energy, mining, oil and gas

### ENR-01 Kenya Power: "Nuru" chatbot and digital channels
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Kenya Power and Lighting Company (KPLC), Kenya, electricity utility, launched October 2025.
- **What the AI does:** Nuru is Kenya Power's first AI chatbot, on its website and its KenyaPowerCare Facebook page, answering customer questions around the clock. Customers can report outages, get account information and pass to a human care agent in real time.
- **Result:** In the financial year ending 30 June 2025, calls to the contact centre fell by 900,000, from 5.2 million to 4.3 million. The press attributes the fall to digital engagement in general (app, USSD, web), not to the chatbot, which launched after that year ended. No published result for Nuru itself.
- **Themes:** S5, S12, PEOPLE (contact-centre agents; human handover kept).
- **Sources:**
  - "Kenya Power boosts customer service with AI tools", The Standard (Kenya), 6 Oct 2025, https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/sports/amp/business/article/2001531235/kenya-power-boosts-customer-service-with-ai-tools
  - "Kenya Power Introduces AI Chatbot and Redesigned App to Improve Customer Service", TechAfrica News, 8 Oct 2025, https://techafricanews.com/2025/10/08/kenya-power-introduces-ai-chatbot-and-redesigned-app-to-improve-customer-service/
  - Company announcement (blocked automated reading; title and date confirmed via search): Kenya Power newsroom, https://newsroom.kplc.co.ke/articles/kenya-power-unveils-revamped-digital-customer-engagement-platforms-and-its-first-ai-chatbot
- **Confidence:** High (two independent outlets agree on launch and features).
- **Caution:** The call reduction is for the year before Nuru launched; do not credit it to the chatbot. Use the case as "a regional utility moving to digital self-service", not as a measured AI win. Nothing comparable found for Umeme or UEDCL in Uganda.

### ENR-02 M-KOPA: machine learning to forecast repayment on every loan, daily
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** M-KOPA, Kenya (also Uganda, Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa), pay-as-you-go asset finance (solar, then smartphones), 2023 feature.
- **What the AI does:** Machine learning models forecast the repayment of every loan in the portfolio every day, giving early warning when a customer's payment pattern changes. This guides pricing and which customers get follow-on products.
- **Result:** No percentage published. The company says AI produced significant increases in repayment performance, especially for follow-on products. Context figures from the same source: "more than 3 million customers"; "more than $1 billion in credit" by 2022; over 12,400 sales representatives and employees in Kenya.
- **Themes:** S2, S7 (runs entirely on Microsoft Azure cloud), S14 (daily payment data is the asset), PEOPLE (large agent sales force).
- **Sources:**
  - "Improving lives with AI, digital and financial inclusion in Kenya, one smartphone at a time", Microsoft Source EMEA, 9 Nov 2023, https://news.microsoft.com/source/emea/features/improving-lives-with-ai-and-digital-and-financial-inclusion-in-kenya-one-smartphone-at-a-time/
- **Confidence:** Medium (one source, written by M-KOPA's cloud vendor).
- **Caution:** Vendor-partner feature; no independent figure on accuracy or repayment uplift. Figures date from 2022-2023. No equivalent published AI claim found for Sun King, whose credit checks are described as agent-led.

### ENR-03 Shell: predictive maintenance on 10,000+ pieces of equipment (with C3 AI)
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Shell, global (upstream, manufacturing, integrated gas), oil and gas; partnership since 2018, milestone March 2022.
- **What the AI does:** Sensors on control valves, pumps, compressors and other critical equipment feed models that predict when a machine is likely to fail. Engineers can then fix it before it breaks.
- **Result:** More than 10,000 pieces of equipment monitored; about 20 billion rows of data a week from more than 3 million sensors; nearly 11,000 machine-learning models making more than 15 million predictions a day (March 2022). No savings figure in the trade-press account. Vendor-reported 2026 update: more than 13,000 pieces of equipment and "hundreds of millions of dollars" of value (vendor claim, not verified).
- **Themes:** S2, S6 (long vendor partnership), S7 (bought platform, on Azure), S12, S14 (sensor data at scale).
- **Sources:**
  - "Shell Scales Predictive Maintenance to 10,000 Pieces", Journal of Petroleum Technology (SPE), 9 Mar 2022, https://jpt.spe.org/shell-scales-predictive-maintenance-to-10-000-pieces
  - C3 AI press release, 8 Mar 2022 (blocked automated reading; consistent with JPT), https://c3.ai/news/shell-achieves-major-milestone-scales-artificial-intelligence-predictive-maintenance-to-10000-pieces-of-equipment-using-c3-ai
- **Confidence:** High for scale figures (vendor release plus trade-body journal); value claims are vendor-only.
- **Caution:** Figures originate with the vendor. Teach the scale and the data foundation, not the dollar value.

### ENR-04 Rio Tinto: autonomous haul trucks in the Pilbara iron-ore mines
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Rio Tinto, Australia, mining; figures for 2017, reported January 2018.
- **What the AI does:** Driverless haul trucks, controlled by an autonomous haulage system and supervised from a remote operations centre, carry ore and waste around the mine. The trucks do not need breaks or shift changes.
- **Result:** In 2017 each autonomous truck operated on average about 700 hours more than a conventional truck, with about 15% lower load and haul unit costs. The autonomous fleet passed one billion tonnes hauled and moved roughly a quarter of total Pilbara material in 2017. About a fifth of Rio's roughly 400 Pilbara haul trucks were autonomous at the time.
- **Themes:** S1 (machine perception of the mine), S11 (machines that act, with remote supervision), S12, PEOPLE (driver roles shift to remote operation and maintenance).
- **Sources:**
  - "Rio Tinto reaches autonomous truck milestone", Computerworld, 30 Jan 2018, https://www.computerworld.com/article/1653222/rio-tinto-reaches-autonomous-truck-milestone.html
  - Rio Tinto 2014 Form 6-K (earlier, qualitative benefits: cycle times, tyre life, fuel, maintenance), US SEC, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000863064/000100329714000234/es99-2.htm (seen in search results; not opened)
- **Confidence:** Medium-High (company figures reported by a major trade outlet; the original Rio Tinto release URL now returns "page not found").
- **Caution:** Company-reported 2017 figures; later blogs quoting "2026" numbers could not be traced to Rio Tinto and are excluded. Safety benefit is claimed but not quantified in the source.

### ENR-05 Octopus Energy: AI-drafted customer emails, then an AI email agent ("Arlo")
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Octopus Energy, UK, energy retail; 2023 claim and 2026 trial.
- **What the AI does:** Generative AI drafts or sends replies to customer emails. In the 2026 "Arlo" trial, the AI answered some emails itself, while sensitive cases, complex complaints and vulnerable customers stayed with human staff.
- **Result:** 2023 (CEO claim in The Times): AI was doing the work of about 250 people answering emails; customer satisfaction 80% for AI-assisted emails versus 65% for humans; CEO said job cuts unlikely. 2026 trial (company): Arlo handled about 8,000 emails a week, about 4% of UK customer emails, over three months, with satisfaction of 76% versus 72% for comparable human responses. Every AI email is labelled and customers can ask for a human.
- **Themes:** S3 (comparing AI vs human quality), S5, S7 (built in-house on its own Kraken platform), S9, S12, S13 (labelling, vulnerable-customer exclusion), PEOPLE.
- **Sources:**
  - "Octopus Energy's AI trial wins customer approval", Octopus Energy press release, 7 Jul 2026, https://octopus.energy/press/more-news-press-releases/octopus-energy-s-ai-trial-wins-customer-approval/
  - "AI doing the work of over 200 people at Octopus, chief executive says", City A.M., 8 May 2023, https://www.cityam.com/?p=1999839
- **Confidence:** High (company release plus independent report of the CEO's article).
- **Caution:** Both figures are company-reported. The 2023 and 2026 numbers describe different systems; do not combine them.

## B. Retail, logistics and transport

### RTL-01 Air Canada: the company is liable for what its chatbot says (Moffatt v Air Canada)
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Air Canada, Canada, airline; advice given November 2022, ruling February 2024.
- **What the AI does:** A website chatbot answered a bereaved passenger's question about bereavement fares. It wrongly told him he could claim the discount retroactively within 90 days; the real policy required a request before travel.
- **Result:** The British Columbia Civil Resolution Tribunal (2024 BCCRT 149) rejected Air Canada's argument that the chatbot was a separate legal entity responsible for its own actions, holding that the chatbot is part of the airline's website. Air Canada was ordered to pay about CAD 812 in damages, interest and fees (claim was CAD 880).
- **Themes:** S5, S10, S13 (liability for automated customer communications).
- **Sources:**
  - "Air Canada chatbot gave a passenger bad advice..." CBS News, Feb 2024, https://www.cbsnews.com/news/aircanada-chatbot-discount-customer
  - "BC Tribunal Confirms Companies Remain Liable for Information Provided by AI Chatbot", American Bar Association Business Law Today, Feb 2024, https://www.americanbar.org/groups/business_law/resources/business-law-today/2024-february/bc-tribunal-confirms-companies-remain-liable-information-provided-ai-chatbot/ (blocked automated reading; consistent with search summary)
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Caution:** Small-claims tribunal, so the sum is tiny; the lesson is liability and reputation, not the amount.

### RTL-02 DPD: customer-service chatbot swears and criticises the company
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** DPD, UK, parcel delivery, January 2024.
- **What the AI does:** DPD's chat assistant combined scripted answers with a generative AI element. After a system update, a frustrated customer got it to swear, write a poem criticising DPD and call itself useless.
- **Result:** DPD said an error occurred after a system update, immediately disabled the AI element and began updating it. No business figure published; the screenshots spread widely on social media.
- **Themes:** S5, S10, S13 (testing and change control before updates).
- **Sources:**
  - "DPD Disables AI Chatbot After It Swears At Customer", Silicon UK, 22 Jan 2024, https://www.silicon.co.uk/e-innovation/artificial-intelligence/dpd-disable-ai-chatbot-546650
  - Also reported by BBC and The Guardian (20 Jan 2024; not opened).
- **Confidence:** High (widely reported, company statement quoted).
- **Caution:** Reputational incident, no financial loss reported.

### RTL-03 McDonald's and IBM: AI drive-through ordering test ended
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** McDonald's with IBM, USA, quick-service restaurants; October 2021 to mid-2024.
- **What the AI does:** Voice AI took customers' orders at the drive-through instead of a crew member. It had to understand speech in noisy settings, across accents.
- **Result:** Tested at about 100 restaurants; McDonald's ended the partnership on this product in June 2024. A 2022 analyst report put ordering accuracy in the low 80% range against McDonald's target of 95%-plus before wider rollout; viral videos of wrong orders drew attention. McDonald's said voice ordering will still be part of its future.
- **Themes:** S1 (speech), S3 (accuracy threshold before scaling), S6 (vendor choice), S10, PEOPLE (crew roles).
- **Sources:**
  - "McDonald's is ending its AI drive-thru test with IBM", Nation's Restaurant News, 17 Jun 2024, https://www.nrn.com/quick-service/mcdonald-s-is-ending-its-ai-drive-thru-test-with-ibm
  - "McDonald's is ending its test run of AI-powered drive-thrus with IBM", Associated Press via ABC News, 18 Jun 2024, https://abcnews.go.com/US/wireStory/mcdonalds-ending-test-run-ai-powered-drive-thrus-111236274 (seen in search; not opened)
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Caution:** The "low 80%" accuracy figure is an analyst estimate (BTIG, 2022), not a McDonald's figure.

### RTL-04 Amazon: AI demand forecasting model
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Amazon, USA, Canada, Mexico, Brazil; e-commerce and logistics; announced 11 June 2025.
- **What the AI does:** A forecasting model predicts what customers will want, where and when, for hundreds of millions of products each day, using sales history plus things like weather and holidays. Inventory is placed nearer to expected demand.
- **Result:** Company states a 10% improvement in long-term national forecasts for deal events and a 20% improvement in regional forecasts for millions of popular items.
- **Themes:** S2, S3 (forecast accuracy as the KPI), S12, S14.
- **Sources:**
  - "Amazon AI innovations: delivery, forecasting, robotics", About Amazon, 11 Jun 2025, https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/operations/amazon-ai-innovations-delivery-forecasting-robotics
  - Retail Dive / Supply Chain Dive coverage, June 2025, https://www.supplychaindive.com/news/amazon-ai-supply-chain-usage-upgrades/750713/ (seen in search; not opened)
- **Confidence:** Medium (company source; trade press repeats it).
- **Caution:** Self-reported; the baseline and measurement method are not published.

### RTL-05 Jumia: AI workflows and headcount reduction
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Jumia, pan-African e-commerce (including Kenya, Uganda, Nigeria), 2025-2026.
- **What the AI does:** Jumia says it is using AI-driven workflows in customer service, marketing, technology operations and other internal work. The aim is lower operating costs on the path to profitability.
- **Result:** Headcount fell about 7% from 31 Dec 2024 to just over 2,000 by 30 Sep 2025, tied by the company to AI-driven cost optimisation. Q3 2025: general and administrative costs down 7% to USD 17.6 million; operating loss down 13% to USD 17.4 million (not attributed to AI specifically). May 2026: plans to cut about 200 more jobs (roughly 10%) as AI tools roll out; headcount down from 4,318 at end-2022.
- **Themes:** S5, S11, S12, PEOPLE (job reductions in customer service and back office).
- **Sources:**
  - "Jumia Slashes Staff To 2,000, Bets on AI to Turn Its Business Profitable", WeeTracker, 14 Nov 2025, https://weetracker.com/2025/11/14/jumia-slashes-staff-to-2000-bets-on-ai-to-turn-its-business-profitable/

  - "Jumia narrows losses, cuts 7% staff as AI reshapes operations", TechCabal, Nov 2025, https://techcabal.com/?p=173346 (blocked automated reading)
- **Confidence:** High for headcount; cost savings are not separated out for AI.
- **Caution:** A claim that "over half" of customer interactions run through conversational AI appeared in one secondary source only and is excluded. Do not say AI caused the loss reduction.

### RTL-06 Kenya Airways: staff assistant, data lake, AI policy approved by the board
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Kenya Airways, Kenya, airline, 2025-2026.
- **What the AI does:** More than 1,000 staff use an AWS assistant (Amazon Quick) for daily work; AI speeds the review of maintenance documents; a data lake gives each customer one identifier so disrupted travellers can be spotted early. Separately (May 2026) the airline adopted an AI fare-pricing platform (FlyNava Jupiter 5.0).
- **Result:** No measured figure. The CIO says tasks that took days or weeks now take hours. The data lake is described as still under construction.
- **Themes:** S2 (pricing), S6, S7 (cloud vendor), S8, S13 (dedicated data and AI policy approved by the board; each use case is checked against it), S14, PEOPLE.
- **Sources:**
  - "Inside Kenya Airways' AI playbook", CIO Africa, 31 Aug 2026, https://cioafrica.co/inside-kenya-airways-ai-playbook/ (interview with CIO Fred Kitunga at AWS Summit Johannesburg)

### RTL-07 Uber Eats: facial-recognition ID check and a discrimination claim
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Uber Eats, UK, delivery platform; removal 2021, settlement March 2024.
- **What the AI does:** Couriers must pass a real-time selfie check that compares their face to the account photo. After "continued mismatches", a Black courier was removed from the platform through an automated process.
- **Result:** The courier, funded by the Equality and Human Rights Commission and his union, brought claims of indirect race discrimination, harassment and victimisation, and received an undisclosed settlement in March 2024; he was reinstated. Uber says checks have "robust human review" and that the automated check did not cause the removal. The bias question was never tested in court.
- **Themes:** S1, S3 (false matches), S10, S11 (automated deactivation), S13 (right to explanation and appeal), PEOPLE (gig workers managed by algorithm).
- **Sources:**
  - "Uber Eats settles driver's facial recognition discrimination claim", Personnel Today, 26 Mar 2024, https://www.personneltoday.com/hr/uber-eats-settles-drivers-facial-recognition-discrimination-claim
  - Biometric Update, Mar 2024, https://www.biometricupdate.com/202403/uber-eats-settles-drivers-biometric-id-verification-discrimination-case (seen in search; not opened)
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Caution:** Settled without admission. Name only the organisation in class, not the individual; the brief bars personal data on private individuals.

## C. Education

### EDU-01 Edo State, Nigeria: GPT-4 after-school tutoring, randomised trial (World Bank)
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** World Bank with Edo State education authorities, Nigeria, public senior secondary schools; pilot June-July 2024, published 2025.
- **What the AI does:** Students used Microsoft Copilot (GPT-4) as a virtual English tutor in an after-school programme, working in pairs with teachers present. The trial compared them with students randomly left out.
- **Result:** Gains of about 0.3 standard deviations over six weeks, described as nearly two years of typical learning, and better than about 80% of interventions in a database of developing-country trials (World Bank blog, Jan 2025). The working paper gives 0.31 SD on a combined test (English, AI knowledge, digital skills) and 0.23 SD on English alone; nine schools.
- **Themes:** S3 (reading an effect size properly), S5, S12, PEOPLE (teacher role in supervising AI use).
- **Sources:**
  - "From chalkboards to chatbots: Transforming learning in Nigeria, one prompt at a time", World Bank Blogs, 9 Jan 2025, https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/education/From-chalkboards-to-chatbots-Transforming-learning-in-Nigeria
  - "From Chalkboards to Chatbots: Evaluating the Impact of Generative AI on Learning Outcomes in Nigeria", World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 11125 (2025), reproducibility catalogue https://reproducibility.worldbank.org/catalog/419 (figures 0.31 / 0.23 from search summary of this catalogue; paper PDF not opened)
- **Confidence:** High (randomised trial, primary publisher).
- **Caution:** Six-week pilot. "Two years of learning" is a comparison, not a measurement. Full-year projections in the paper are model-based extrapolations.

### EDU-02 Rising Academies "Rori": WhatsApp maths tutor in Ghana
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Rising Academies, Ghana, private school network; study 2023, paper Feb/May 2024.
- **What the AI does:** Rori is a chat-based maths tutor delivered through WhatsApp on basic phones. Students used it for two 30-minute sessions a week for 8 months on top of normal lessons.
- **Result:** About 1,000 students in grades 3-9 across 11 schools; effect size 0.37 on maths growth scores (p < 0.001), year 1 only.
- **Themes:** S5, S12 (low-bandwidth delivery).
- **Sources:**
  - Henkel et al., "Effective and Scalable Math Support: Evidence on the Impact of an AI-Tutor on Math Achievement in Ghana", arXiv:2402.09809, v2 5 May 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09809
- **Confidence:** Medium (one study; authors linked to the provider; preprint, later in a Springer conference volume).
- **Caution:** Not a randomised trial at student level (schools assigned to arms); year-1 results only; authors are affiliated with Rising Academies.

### EDU-03 Harvard: purpose-built AI physics tutor versus active-learning class
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Harvard University, USA, higher education; trial fall 2023, Gazette Sept 2024, Scientific Reports June 2025.
- **What the AI does:** Instructors built a tutor on the GPT API with lesson-specific instructions so it coached like an experienced teacher instead of giving answers. Students did one lesson with the tutor at home and one in an active-learning class, then switched.
- **Result:** 194 students. Learning gains in the AI-tutored condition were about double those in the in-class condition (preliminary analysis reported by Harvard). Published version: Kestin et al., Scientific Reports 15, 17458 (2025).
- **Themes:** S3, S5, S9 (tutor grounded in the course's own material and design), S12, PEOPLE (teacher time freed for in-class work).
- **Sources:**
  - "Professor tailored AI tutor to physics course. Engagement doubled.", Harvard Gazette, 5 Sep 2024, https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2024/09/professor-tailored-ai-tutor-to-physics-course-engagement-doubled/
  - Kestin, Miller et al., "AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning...", Scientific Reports, June 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-97652-6 (journal page blocked automated reading; publication details from search)
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Caution:** One course, two topics, short-term learning, highly selective students. The tutor was carefully designed, not off-the-shelf ChatGPT.

### EDU-04 EIDU (Kenya): AI-generated tutoring plans in public primary classrooms
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** EIDU, Kenya, edtech for pre-primary and primary schools; J-PAL-supported pilot, 2025.
- **What the AI does:** A generative AI model built with Google.org support spots each child's learning gaps and produces short (about 15-minute) tutoring plans for small groups. Teachers use the plans for catch-up within EIDU's structured lessons.
- **Result:** No published result for the AI tool. EIDU's wider structured-pedagogy approach shows preliminary literacy and numeracy gains of 0.5 standard deviations; a three-arm randomised evaluation is planned.
- **Themes:** S2, S5, S14 (classroom data on each child), PEOPLE (teacher workload).
- **Sources:**
  - "An AI-powered Structured Pedagogy Program's Impact on Student Learning and Teacher Productivity", J-PAL, 2025, https://www.povertyactionlab.org/initiative-project/ai-powered-structured-pedagogy-programs-impact-student-learning-and-teacher
- **Confidence:** Medium.
- **Caution:** Pilot only. The 0.5 SD figure is for the non-AI programme. Use as "East African case to watch", not as evidence. No rigorous East African AI-tutor trial was found.

### EDU-05 Georgia State University: "Pounce" admissions chatbot cuts summer melt
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Georgia State University with AdmitHub (now Mainstay), USA; randomised trial in 2016.
- **What the AI does:** A text-message chatbot answered admitted students' questions on financial aid, forms and deadlines and nudged them through enrolment steps. Staff dealt only with questions it could not handle.
- **Result:** Of more than 7,000 admitted students, half got the chatbot. Summer melt (admitted students who never enrol) was 21.4% lower than in the control group, and enrolment 3.9% higher. Less than 1% of over 50,000 student messages needed staff attention.
- **Themes:** S3, S5, S11, S12, PEOPLE (admissions staff focus on hard cases).
- **Sources:**
  - "Using AI Chatbots to Freeze 'Summer Melt' in Higher Ed", Campus Technology, 7 Mar 2017, https://campustechnology.com/articles/2017/03/07/using-ai-chatbots-to-freeze-summer-melt-in-higher-ed.aspx
  - Vendor case study (gives 3.3% enrolment figure for priority-deadline students), Mainstay, https://mainstay.com/case_study/how-georgia-state-university-supports-every-student-with-personalized-text-messaging/ (seen in search; not opened)
- **Confidence:** Medium-High (randomised trial run by independent academics Page and Gehlbach; the peer-reviewed paper was not opened).

### LGL-01 Mata v Avianca: lawyers fined for citing cases invented by ChatGPT
- **Organisation / country / industry / year:** Levidow, Levidow & Oberman P.C., USA (S.D.N.Y.), law firm; sanctions 22 June 2023.
- **What the AI does:** A lawyer used ChatGPT for legal research, and it produced court decisions that do not exist, with fake quotations. When asked, ChatGPT insisted one case "does indeed exist".

The court found bad faith partly because they did not admit the problem when first challenged (March 2023), only after an order to show cause (May 2023).
