EAST AFRICA STAPLES BULLETIN, Issue 41 (invented publisher: Savanna Market Watch)
Topic: maize flour and cooking oil, Eastern Uganda wholesale, week 41

Maize flour
- The average wholesale price of maize flour in Eastern Uganda was UGX 3,400 per kg in week 41, about 2% above week 40.
- Supply from the new harvest is arriving slowly. Millers in the region report running at about 70% of capacity.
- Demand is steady from schools and small shops. Large buyers are waiting for lower prices in November.

Cooking oil
- Refined cooking oil averaged UGX 5,200 per litre at wholesale, unchanged from the previous week.
- Traders expect little movement before the festive season.

What to watch
- Road conditions around Mbale after the heavy rains, which can delay trucks by one or two days.
- The exchange rate, because most palm oil is imported.

Summary view of this bulletin: prices are stable to slightly higher, and supply is the main risk for maize flour. These notes are written for a training exercise and the figures are invented.
