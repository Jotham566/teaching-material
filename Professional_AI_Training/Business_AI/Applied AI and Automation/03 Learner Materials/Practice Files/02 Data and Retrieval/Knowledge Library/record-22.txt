VICTORIA SERVICES LTD - INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY (EXTRACT)
Section 7: Access for external support staff
Issued 4 March 2026, owner: IT Lead

7.1 External support staff may only use accounts set up in their own names. Shared logins are not allowed.
7.2 Each request for access must name the individual, the system, the purpose and the dates. It is recorded on a support access request form.
7.3 The IT lead assesses the request. The finance manager approves access to systems holding financial data.
7.4 Access is granted for the period requested and no longer. It lapses automatically on the end date.
7.5 Remote sessions go through the company VPN and are logged.
7.6 If an external support person leaves their employer or changes role, the supplier must tell the IT lead within five working days so that the account can be closed.
7.7 Access logs are reviewed monthly by the IT lead.

Questions about this policy go to itlead@victoriaservices.example.
