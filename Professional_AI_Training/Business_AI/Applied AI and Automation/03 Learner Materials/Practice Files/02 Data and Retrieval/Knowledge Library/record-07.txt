Background
Management has been approached by a software company based overseas offering a replacement reporting tool and a support plan. The tool is cheaper to license than our current set up and has a more modern dashboard. Their sales contact has said their team could provide remote support from outside Uganda and invoice us in US dollars.

Estimated cost
Annual licence and support fee: to be confirmed. The vendor has promised a written quotation by mid November.

Open points

- Data hosting location
- Anything we owe on payments abroad
- How this fits with the existing Equator arrangement

No tax analysis has been done at this stage. The finance manager will raise it with the firm.

Recommendation
Note the proposal and authorise management to obtain the quotation. No commitment to be made.
