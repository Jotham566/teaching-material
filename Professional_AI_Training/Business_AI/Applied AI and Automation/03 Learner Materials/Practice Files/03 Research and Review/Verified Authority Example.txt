Appeal scope: the reproduced judgment’s printed page 3, lines 24–27 cites section 27(2) in the 7th Revised Edition. Short quote: An appeal to the High Court may be made on questions of law only.
Substantive reading: Ground 5 (printed pages 22 to 24) challenges VAT on the 10% programme delivery fee deducted by Goal Ireland. The court found that Goal Ireland fundraised for the appellant abroad and secured funding for it, that this was a service provided by a person outside Uganda to a person in Uganda, and so an import of services, and that VAT on it was correctly assessed. The court also cites section 5(1)(c) of the VAT Act (Cap. 344, 7th Revised Edition) for the rule that the recipient of imported services is liable to collect the tax. The orders (printed page 24) affirm the Tribunal, dismiss the appeal and award costs. This is a historical holding between other parties. Use the court’s reasoning, not a summary. Do not infer Victoria’s tax position from it.
Edition reference: the judgment cites section 27(2) in the 7th Revised Edition (Cap 341). A section number belongs to an edition. Before relying on it in advice, read the provision in the current consolidated Act and record the edition and the date you checked. Do not assume renumbering from a difference in numbers alone.
