FICTIONAL TEACHING DRAFT. UNSIGNED AND SUPERSEDED.
CONTRACT-01 v1, draft dated 30 June 2026, between Equator Systems Ltd (Provider) and Victoria Services Ltd (Customer).
This draft used the same Common Paper Cloud Service Agreement Standard Terms, Version 3.0, as the executed agreement CONTRACT-01 v2. The only provision that differs from v2 in this teaching set is the Key Terms entry "Other Changes to Standard Terms", which in this draft read:
"12.6 Assignment.  Neither party may assign any rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party. In addition, Provider must obtain Customer’s prior written consent before any change of control or reorganization of Provider takes effect. Any attempted but non-permitted assignment is void. This Agreement will be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties and their permitted successors and assigns."
The draft contained no sentence about support staff access. The executed v2 limits the consent requirement to a change of control, excludes a reorganization within a wholly owned group when Provider remains responsible, and adds the support staff approval sentence. Do not treat this draft as the current agreement.
