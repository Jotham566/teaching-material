FICTIONAL CUSTOMER-SIDE PLAYBOOK v3
The firm acts for Victoria Services Ltd, customer.
1. Read the Order Form and Key Terms before the Standard Terms. The Key Terms control over the Standard Terms. Identify every change the Key Terms make and the precise condition of any exception.
2. Our teaching position accepts a group-reorganisation exception to the change-of-control consent only while Provider remains responsible for its obligations. This is a negotiated customer preference, not mandatory law.
3. Treat any assignment of the agreement as requiring Victoria’s prior written consent. Do not confuse an assignment with a change of control.
4. Consider a notice requirement for a qualifying reorganisation. Propose the period and recipient for agreement; do not invent agreed terms.
5. Support access needs named staff and Victoria’s prior written approval (Section 1.5 as supplemented). Confidentiality applies (Section 10).
6. Obtain the signed Fee Schedule before concluding on fees, the liability cap (Section 8.1 and the Key Terms cap of 1x fees), service credits or renewal.
7. Check data terms: Section 3.1 requires a data processing agreement only for GDPR data, and none is attached. Ask what protects personal data under Ugandan law.
8. Check tax terms: Section 4.2 makes Customer responsible for taxes the Provider itemizes, including withholding. Ask who bears withholding and VAT.
9. Every proposed amendment requires a source, a rationale and partner review.
These are teaching preferences, not statements of mandatory law.
