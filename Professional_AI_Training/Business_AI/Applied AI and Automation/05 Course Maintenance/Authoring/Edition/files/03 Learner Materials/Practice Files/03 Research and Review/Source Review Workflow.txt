SOURCE REVIEW WORKFLOW
I operate the tool; we check the answer together in class.
1. State the decision, source purpose and intended document identity. Use the complete current agreement for obligations; keep correspondence as factual evidence.
2. Ask for established facts, unresolved facts, relevant source locations and a conditional conclusion. An unknown transaction trigger must remain unresolved.
3. Open every cited passage. Copy its wording from the source rather than trusting a model's recreated quotation. Preserve negation, conditions, exceptions, headings and amendments.
4. Inspect related provisions. Separate an assignment from a change of control, the trigger from an exception, and contractual text from a proposed amendment or researched legal conclusion.
5. Mark each claim supported, unsupported or unresolved. A source location alone does not pass this check.
6. Recalculate figures outside the language model. Keep distinct totals and statutory editions separate.
7. Hold the draft if an important claim fails. Revise with the failure stated explicitly, then check the revision again. Save both outputs and label feedback-assisted revisions.
8. Release only the reviewed working record. Save its sources, unresolved questions, reviewer and date. This classroom process does not certify a production system or replace the firm's approval process.
