Date: 18 March 2025
Author: Sarah Nakato

As part of onboarding Victoria Services as a client, we asked Grace Atim what she knew about the ownership of Equator Systems Ltd. She described the following structure, taken from Equator's brochure and the tender pack:

Equator Systems Ltd is wholly owned by Equator Holdings (Mauritius) Ltd. That company is owned 70 percent by the founder and 30 percent by a regional investment fund. Equator Systems Ltd has one operating subsidiary, Equator Support Services Ltd, which employs the support engineers.

She was not sure about the dates of the brochure and has no signed confirmation. We have not checked the registry.

Action: Grace to ask Equator whether the brochure is up to date. No answer recorded.

This note records only what Grace told us on the day. It is a working description and may need amending.
