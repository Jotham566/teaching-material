RESTRICTED - MATTER OTHER-02 ONLY

Change of control

If Lakeview Data Ltd undergoes a change of control, whether through a sale of shares, a merger or the transfer of voting rights, Lakeview shall give Kisumu Freight Ltd not less than sixty days' written notice before the change takes effect. Kisumu Freight's consent to the change shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed, and Kisumu shall give or refuse its consent in writing within thirty days of receiving the notice together with reasonable details of the new controlling party. If consent is refused, Kisumu may end the agreement on ninety days' written notice without penalty.

In this clause, 'control' means the power to direct the management of Lakeview Data Ltd, whether through shares, voting rights or arrangement.
