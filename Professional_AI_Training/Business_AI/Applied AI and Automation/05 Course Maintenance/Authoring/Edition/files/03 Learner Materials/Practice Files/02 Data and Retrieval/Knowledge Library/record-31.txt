KAMPALA LEGAL AND TAX - DRAFT PLAYBOOK NOTE
Topic: how we handle a service provider telling a client about a change in its group
Version 1, 12 November 2025, for discussion only

Working position
1. Where a client's provider announces any internal change, the associate should first record what the provider has said and when.
2. The associate should collect the agreement and any amendments, and list what is missing.
3. Send a short email to the client explaining that we will review the position and that they should not respond to the provider until we have done so.
4. Raise with the partner within two working days.

Open question at this stage: whether a one page checklist is enough or whether a longer template is needed. Peter Mugisha prefers the one page.
