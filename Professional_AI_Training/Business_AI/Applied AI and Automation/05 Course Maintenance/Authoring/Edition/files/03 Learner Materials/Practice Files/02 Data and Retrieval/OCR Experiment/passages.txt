Provider must obtain Customer’s prior written consent before a change of control of Provider takes effect. That consent is not required for a reorganization within a wholly owned group if Provider remains responsible for its obligations under this Agreement.

Neither party may assign any rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party. However, either party may assign this Agreement upon notice if the assigning party undergoes a merger, change of control, reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all its equity, business, or assets to which this Agreement relates.

The provider shall not access customer data except as needed to provide the service. Named support staff may access customer records only after the customer has approved each person in writing.

Fees are payable within thirty days of the invoice date. No late fee applies unless the customer fails to pay an undisputed amount within ten days after written notice of non-payment.

Either party may terminate this agreement for a material breach that is not cured within thirty days after written notice, unless the breach cannot be cured.

The provider’s total liability for all claims arising out of this agreement shall not exceed the fees paid in the twelve months before the first event giving rise to liability. This limit does not apply to fraud or to a breach of the confidentiality obligations in section 9.

The customer shall not disclose the provider’s confidential information to any third party except its professional advisers who are bound by a duty of confidence, or as required by law after notice to the provider where the law allows notice.

The provider will process personal data only on the customer’s documented instructions and will not transfer personal data outside Uganda unless the customer has given written consent or the destination country has adequate protection.

Service credits of five percent of the monthly fee apply for each full hour of unavailability above four hours in a month. Credits are the customer’s only remedy for unavailability unless the unavailability exceeds forty-eight hours.

This agreement renews for successive periods of twelve months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty days before the end of the current period. The renewal fee shall not exceed the previous fee by more than ten percent.

The provider may use subcontractors to perform the services but remains responsible for their acts and omissions. The provider shall not subcontract the hosting of customer records without the customer’s prior written approval.

Any notice must be in writing and sent to the address stated in the order form. A notice sent by email is treated as received on the next working day, and a notice sent by post two working days after posting.
