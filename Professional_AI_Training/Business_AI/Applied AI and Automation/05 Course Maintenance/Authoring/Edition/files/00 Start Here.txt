APPLIED AI AND AUTOMATION
12 sessions of 2 hours | 24 hours total | flexible schedule

Build on existing use of AI assistants. Use the Guide’s professional decisions
and evidence checks to set the pace; routine interface instructions are private
facilitator preparation. Explain unfamiliar mechanisms before applying them.

01 Course Overview
One current one-page summary to share with the learner.

02 Class Slides
One editable PowerPoint per session, numbered in teaching order.
The essential sequence ends with a check of understanding. Optional exploration
slides follow that check and are labelled in the footer; they are not required
within the two-hour lesson. The set has 233 essential and 52 optional slides.
88 slides explain concepts; the others support demonstrations, discussion,
application, recall and firm decisions. Avoid treating the set as 285 lectures.
Animation Guide.txt lists the native click reveals. Use Slide Show mode.

03 Learner Materials
Learner Workbook.docx and Practice Files/. One fictional legal and tax matter
connects the course. Complete required work together in class; no homework.
Use the blank in-class progress record to identify concepts to revisit.

04 Facilitator Materials
Facilitator Guide.docx gives each 120-minute plan, teaching landmarks,
misconceptions, questions, expected answers and recovery routes.
Every slide has speaker notes written as a script to read aloud. Sources and click counts are in Teaching Resources/Presenter Notes Backup.txt.
Law Firm Implementation Playbook.docx supports one proposed pilot and includes
a completed teaching example, costs, risks, owners and a staged adoption route.
Teaching Resources/ contains instructor answer keys, demonstration preparation
and saved actual runs. Keep answer keys separate from learner handouts.

BEFORE CLASS
Read Teaching Resources/Demonstration Preflight.txt and Run Demonstrations.txt.
Rehearse the chosen AI account and any connected tools. Actual saved runs,
constructed examples and local simulations are labelled separately. The local
workflow page works without an account. It does not connect to URA or file tax.
The selected portal builder, enterprise search and live deep research still
need account-specific rehearsal. Use the documented fallback if unavailable.

05 Course Maintenance
Review Response.txt maps the independent feedback to the changes.
Benchmark Review.txt records the public-syllabus comparison and its limits.
Authoring/ preserves editable sources. Validation/ records checks and review
scope. This is maintenance material, not another teaching edition.
