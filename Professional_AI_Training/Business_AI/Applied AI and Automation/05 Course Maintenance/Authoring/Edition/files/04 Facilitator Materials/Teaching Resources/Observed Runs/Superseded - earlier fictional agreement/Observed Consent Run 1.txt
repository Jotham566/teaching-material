## Partner-Review Draft: Clause 3 Analysis

**Matter:** M-01 – Victoria Services Ltd / Equator Systems Ltd

### Rule and Exception

Clause 3 states:

> "The customer must give prior written consent to a change of control of the provider. Consent is not required for a reorganisation within a wholly owned group if the provider remains responsible for its obligations."

**General rule:** Prior written consent is required for any change of control of Equator.

**Exception:** Consent is not required where:
1. The transaction is a reorganisation within a wholly owned group; **and**
2. Equator remains responsible for its contractual obligations.

Both conditions must be satisfied for the exception to apply.

### Missing Facts

Before advising whether Victoria's consent is required, we need confirmation of:

1. **Ownership structure** – Whether the proposed reorganisation is genuinely "within a wholly owned group" (we have not received Equator's ownership chart)

2. **Continued responsibility** – Whether Equator will remain the contracting party responsible for its obligations post-reorganisation (no confirmation supplied)

3. **Nature of the transaction** – Whether this is properly characterised as an internal "reorganisation" rather than a transfer to a third party

### Conclusion

We cannot yet advise that the exception applies. Victoria should request Equator's group structure chart and written confirmation that Equator remains responsible for all obligations under CONTRACT-01 v2 following the reorganisation.
