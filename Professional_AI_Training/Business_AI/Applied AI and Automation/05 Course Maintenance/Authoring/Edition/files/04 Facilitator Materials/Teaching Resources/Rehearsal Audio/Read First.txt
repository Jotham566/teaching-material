SYSTEM VOICE PRACTICE SCRIPTS AND TIMING RECORDS
The twelve Session NN.txt files contain the core slide scripts used for the measured synthetic-voice rehearsal. Timing Results.json and the authoring rehearsal_status.json retain the observed durations and provenance. These are automated practice measurements, not a recording of Jotham teaching or a full two-hour class.

The generated M4A files and their duplicate edition snapshot were removed during repository cleanup on 28 October 2026. They are optional local derivatives. PowerPoint notes remain the teaching source; the scripts and timing records stay versioned.

To regenerate one optional recording on the teaching Mac, open Terminal in this directory and run:
/usr/bin/say -v Daniel -r 130 -f "Session 01.txt" -o "$TMPDIR/KLAi-Session-01.aiff"
Change the session number as needed. The output is outside the repository. Voice availability can vary; a new recording is a new measurement.
