TASK GIVEN
An unrelated buyer acquires Equator by a share sale. What must Equator do, and what does the agreement say happens if it does not? Keep the answer under 250 words.

ACTUAL RESPONSE (unedited)
------------------------------------------------------------
What Equator must do

A share sale to an unrelated buyer is a change of control of Provider. Under Teaching Adaptation 1, Section 12.6 now says Equator "must obtain Customer's prior written consent before a change of control of Provider takes effect." So Equator needs Victoria's written consent before completion. Later consent does not satisfy the clause.

The exception does not help. It covers only a reorganization within a wholly owned group where Equator stays responsible. The original wording, which let a party assign on notice after a change of control, was replaced and no longer applies.

What happens if Equator does not obtain consent

The agreement states no specific consequence for a change of control without consent. The "void" sentence covers a "non-permitted assignment". A share sale does not itself assign the agreement, because Equator stays the contracting party. I would not rely on that sentence to say the sale is void.

The likely consequence is breach of Section 12.6. Under Section 5.3, Victoria may then:
- terminate if Equator fails to cure a material breach within 30 days of notice (5.3(a)); or
- terminate on notice if the breach cannot be cured (5.3(b)(i)).

Whether the breach is material or incurable is a judgment on the facts. Section 12.5 keeps other remedies available. Any damages claim would face the General Cap, 1x the Fees in the prior 12 months. The Fee Schedule was not supplied, so I give no figure.
