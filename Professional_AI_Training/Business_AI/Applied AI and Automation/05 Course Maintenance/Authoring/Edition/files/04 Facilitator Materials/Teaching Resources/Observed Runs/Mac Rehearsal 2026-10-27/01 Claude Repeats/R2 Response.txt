**Analysis**

Under the modified Section 12.6 (Teaching Adaptation 1):

> "Neither party may assign any rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party... That consent is not required for a reorganization within a wholly owned group if Provider remains responsible for its obligations under this Agreement."

Victoria's consent is **not required** if both conditions are met:
1. The reorganisation is within a wholly owned group
2. Equator remains responsible for its obligations

**What is unknown:**
- Whether Equator's group entities are wholly owned
- Whether Equator will remain responsible for obligations post-reorganisation
- Whether the Agreement itself will be assigned/transferred

**Questions for Equator:**
1. Provide an ownership chart showing the relevant group entities are wholly owned
2. Confirm in writing that Equator Systems Ltd will remain responsible for all obligations under the Agreement after the reorganisation
3. Clarify whether any assignment or transfer of the Agreement is proposed (which would require consent regardless of the reorganisation exception)
4. Describe the structure of the proposed reorganisation

If Equator cannot satisfy the wholly-owned-group requirement or will not remain responsible, prior written consent is required under Section 12.6.
