CURRICULUM AND PAID-PROGRAMME BENCHMARK
26 October 2026 | Revised after independent evaluations

SCOPE AND CONCLUSION
This review compares public syllabi, published outcomes and official guidance,
not full paid course interiors or equivalent qualifications. The first review
was too reassuring about readiness: coverage and clean layouts did not establish
teaching depth, credible timing or good transfer questions. The revisions below
address those weaknesses. Learning still needs to be observed in the classes.

The course remains 12 sessions of two hours, with flexible scheduling and no
required homework. It now contains 285 slides: 233 in the essential sequence
and 52 clearly marked optional slides after the closing checks. Only
88 slides are allocated to concept explanation; the remaining essential
slides support worked cases, practice, recall and decisions. Each session has
its own 120-minute allocation, including a break and buffer.

WHAT CHANGED
• Generation and reliability is a dedicated session; tax and filing content
  is combined into a bounded evidence-and-control lesson.
• Technical topics requested by the client remain available as optional
  exploration; they were not deleted simply to reduce the slide count.
• The customer/provider roles, share sale and assignment are distinct.
• The notes and guide include misconceptions, model answers and transfer checks.
• Entry and exit explanations are compared; progress statuses start blank.
• The learner workbook uses meeting-style records and contains no session key.
• The example source set has 40 documents, an extended fictional agreement and
  a larger ledger; these are synthetic exercises, not measured firm data.
• Worked policy, register, proposal, costs and relative decision stages support
  the implementation path. Proposed owners/dates require the firm's agreement.

OBSERVED REHEARSALS AND LIMITS
Seven supplied-source AI runs were saved: three consent, missing fee, share sale
and two reconciliation runs. The first financial result mislabelled receipts
as a declared tax basis after an input without file-name boundaries. The clearer
labelled-source rerun corrected that distinction. This contrast has an input
confound and is not a model error-rate benchmark. The failed result is retained.

Apple Vision OCR preserved the operative negation and qualifications in a
synthetically degraded scan. The damaged extract is a separate constructed
exercise. A local lexical search tests version/access filtering, not vectors.
The read-only agent trace records actual model-selected lookup, host checks and
source return; unknown ID and zero-budget requests were held/denied. The injection
test ran against that small allowlist, not an enterprise security environment.

The deterministic workflow is source-derived and tests rejection, stale approval,
recipient limits, call/spend limits and uncertain acknowledgement. It makes no
external call and no tax calculation. Portal builders, firm-connected retrieval
and live deep research still need the intended teaching-account preflight.

SOURCE ACCURACY
The original Goal Relief PDF was downloaded unmodified and read directly,
including printed p. 3 and pp22–24. The source record includes its hash. K-Files
explicitly cites section 28(2) in the 2023 Revised Edition, differing from Goal
Relief's reference. The course teaches resolving the edition/citation discrepancy,
not silently substituting a number. Current applicability and later treatment
remain separate research tasks. The Ugandan legal source map identifies privacy,
registration and professional-communications questions without declaring any
specific AI product compliant.
