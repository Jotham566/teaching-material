Advice to Victoria Services Ltd: CONTRACT-01 v2, stipulated share-sale variant

Short answer: yes. Equator needs your prior written consent before the sale takes effect.

Why
- Teaching Adaptation 1 in the Key Terms replaces Section 12.6. The standard right to assign on notice after a change of control is gone.
- The new 12.6 says Equator "must obtain Customer's prior written consent before a change of control of Provider takes effect."
- A share sale to an unrelated buyer is a change of control. "Change of control" is not defined in the Standard Terms, so we should confirm the buyer will actually gain control.
- The only carve-out is a reorganisation within a wholly owned group where Equator stays responsible. Your stipulated facts put this deal outside any such group, so the carve-out does not apply.
- A share sale does not transfer the agreement itself, so the first sentence of 12.6 (no assignment without consent) is not the trigger. The change-of-control sentence is.

Consequences
- The clause sets no reasonableness test. You may give, refuse or condition consent, and consent must be in writing (Sections 12.2 and 12.9).
- Closing without consent would be a breach. You could give 30 days' notice to cure, or terminate under Section 5.3 if the breach cannot be cured.
- Your damages would be limited by the General Cap of 1x the 12-month Fees. We have not seen the Fee Schedule and have not inferred the amount.

What to confirm before we advise further
1. The buyer's identity, its ultimate owners and any competitor or sanctions concerns.
2. Equator's ownership chart, and proof that the buyer is unrelated and that control passes.
3. The closing date and timetable, so consent can be given or withheld before closing.
4. Whether the agreement will stay with Equator or be moved to anyone else.
5. Written confirmation that Equator remains bound by all its obligations.
6. Plans for support staff access to your data. Teaching Adaptation 2 requires your written approval of each named person.
7. The signed Fee Schedule, to size any exposure.
