## Analysis

**Applicable Provision (Teaching Adaptation 1):**

> "Provider must obtain Customer's prior written consent before a change of control of Provider takes effect. That consent is not required for a reorganization within a wholly owned group if Provider remains responsible for its obligations under this Agreement."

**Answer:** Victoria's consent may or may not be required—it depends on facts not yet confirmed.

The exception to the consent requirement applies only if **both** conditions are met:
1. The reorganization is "within a wholly owned group"
2. Equator "remains responsible for its obligations under this Agreement"

**What is Unknown:**
- Whether the reorganization is within a wholly owned group
- Whether Equator will remain responsible for its obligations post-reorganization
- Whether the Agreement will be assigned or transferred to another entity

**Questions to Ask Equator:**
1. Please provide the ownership chart showing the current and proposed post-reorganization structure.
2. Confirm whether all entities involved are wholly owned within the same group.
3. Confirm in writing that Equator will remain responsible for all obligations under the Agreement after the reorganization.
4. Clarify whether the Agreement will be assigned or transferred to any other entity.

Until Equator provides this information, Victoria cannot confirm whether the wholly-owned-group exception applies and therefore cannot determine whether her consent is required.
