Under the modified Section 12.6 (Teaching Adaptation 1), the general rule is: "Neither party may assign any rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party."

The exception for reorganization within a wholly owned group does not apply here because Equator proposes to assign to another company, not reorganize within its own group.

**Key points:**
1. Assignment requires Victoria's prior written consent
2. Any attempted non-permitted assignment is void
3. The carve-out for group reorganizations only applies if Provider remains responsible and the reorganization is within a wholly owned group—neither condition is satisfied by an assignment to an external company

**Before advising, confirm:**
- Identity and financial standing of the proposed assignee
- Whether the assignee will assume all Provider obligations (support, SLA, security, confidentiality)
- Whether Equator proposes any continuing liability or guarantee
- Any proposed changes to the service or terms
- The assignee's technical capability to operate the Cloud Service
