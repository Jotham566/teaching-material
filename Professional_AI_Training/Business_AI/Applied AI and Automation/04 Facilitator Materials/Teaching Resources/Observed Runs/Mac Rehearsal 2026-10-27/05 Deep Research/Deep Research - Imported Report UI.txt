									162 heading Factual Matrix, Audit Findings, and Assessment Breakdown, Value: 2
										163 text Factual Matrix, Audit Findings, and Assessment Breakdown
									164 text Goal Relief and Development Organisation is an international non-governmental organization registered in Uganda as a company limited by guarantee, functioning as the operating arm of its parent body, GOAL Ireland [cite: 1, 2, 3, 4]. GOAL Ireland, headquartered in Dublin, manages global fundraising, institutional grant procurement, legal compliance, and strategic oversight for its operational branches [cite: 2, 5]. During the audit period, GOAL Ireland negotiated and executed multi-year grant agreements with international donors to fund humanitarian and development projects in Uganda [cite: 2]. Under its operational structure, GOAL Ireland retained 10% of the gross grant funds as a "program delivery fee" or overhead charge before transferring the remaining 90% to Goal Uganda's operational bank accounts [cite: 2, 5]. Additionally, GOAL Ireland centrally procured software licenses from vendors such as Microsoft and Sage The URA determined that the 10% retained fee constituted taxable consideration for cross-border management and technical services provided by a non-resident entity, triggering obligations for Withholding Tax (WHT) and Value Added Tax (VAT) on imported services [cite: 1, 2]. Furthermore, the URA classified the software license reimbursements as taxable royalties subject to WHT and VAT [cite: 2, 5].
