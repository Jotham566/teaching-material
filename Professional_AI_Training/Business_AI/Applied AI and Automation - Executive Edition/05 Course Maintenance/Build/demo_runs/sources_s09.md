# Sources checked for Session 9 (6 November 2026)

- Lewis, Perez, Piktus, Petroni, Karpukhin, Goyal, Kuttler, Lewis, Yih, Rocktaschel, Riedel, Kiela. "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks", NeurIPS 2020. https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401 . Checked 6 Nov 2026: RAG combines a pre-trained language model (parametric memory) with an index of documents reached by a retriever (non-parametric memory).
- Liu, Lin, Hewitt, Paranjape, Bevilacqua, Petroni, Liang. "Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts", TACL 2024 (arXiv July 2023). https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172 . Checked 6 Nov 2026: models tend to do best when the relevant information is at the start or end of a long input and worse when it is in the middle. Slide wording: "can", "in the tests reported there"; no claim about any current product.
- Hu, Shen, Wallis, Allen-Zhu, Li, Wang, Wang, Chen. "LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models", 2021. https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685 . Checked 6 Nov 2026: keeps pretrained weights frozen and trains small added low-rank matrices, which greatly cuts the number of trainable parameters.
- OpenAI, "Optimizing LLM accuracy" guide. https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/optimizing-llm-accuracy . Checked 6 Nov 2026: start with prompt engineering and a set of 20 or more question and answer pairs to measure; use retrieval (context optimisation) when the model lacks knowledge or needs proprietary or current data; use fine-tuning when output is inconsistent or the format or tone is wrong; the methods can be combined; do not adopt them only because they seem sophisticated. Slide wording follows this as one vendor's guidance, not as a rule for all products.
- Anthropic, "Introducing Contextual Retrieval". https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/contextual-retrieval . Checked 6 Nov 2026: chunks can lose the context needed to be retrieved well (their example: a chunk saying revenue grew 3% with no company or period); for a small knowledge base you can put all of it in the prompt.
- Not used (not checked at the original source): any cost, price or accuracy-improvement figure for retrieval or fine-tuning; the Contextual Retrieval percentage improvements; any context-window size for a named product.
