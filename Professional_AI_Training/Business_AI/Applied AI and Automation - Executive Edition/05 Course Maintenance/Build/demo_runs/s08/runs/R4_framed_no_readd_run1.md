**Working (unrounded values carried through, rounded only at the end)**

1. Hourly rate = 1,800,000 / 160 = 11,250
2. Weekday overtime = 12 h x 11,250 x 1.5 = 12 x 16,875 = 202,500
3. Sunday work = 6 h x 11,250 x 2 = 6 x 22,500 = 135,000
4. Unpaid leave deduction = 1,800,000 / 22 x 1 day = 81,818.18
5. Gross pay = 1,800,000 + 202,500 + 135,000 - 81,818.18 = 2,055,681.82
6. Employee pension (5% of gross) = 102,784.09
7. Employer pension (10% of gross) = 205,568.18
8. Advance repayment = 150,000
9. Net pay = 2,055,681.82 - 102,784.09 - 150,000 = 1,802,897.73, which rounds to **UGX 1,802,898**
10. Total cost = 2,055,681.82 + 205,568.18 = 2,261,250.00, which is **UGX 2,261,250**

**Assumptions and unclear rules**

- No rule is contradictory. The leave deduction uses 22 days, while the hourly rate uses 160 hours. These bases differ (22 days x 8 h = 176 h, not 160). I applied each rule exactly as written.
- The advance is taken "from pay after the pension contribution". This gives the same arithmetic as the net pay formula. I treated the advance as not reducing the pension base, so pension is 5% of full gross.
- I assumed there is no tax (such as PAYE), NSSF rules or other deductions beyond those listed.
- I rounded only the final figures. If your payslip rounds each line first, the result may differ by about 1 shilling.
