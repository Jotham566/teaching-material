Answers from the Nile Valley Foods Staff Handbook (Version 4) only.

Q01. 21 working days of paid annual leave per leave year. Clause 2.1.

Q02. At least 14 days before the first day of leave. Clause 2.2.

Q03. The Finance Manager (UGX 1,500,000 falls in the UGX 200,001 to UGX 2,000,000 band). Clause 3.2.

Q04. Within 14 days of the spending. Clause 3.1.

Q05. At 2 times the normal hourly rate, provided the line manager approved the overtime before it was worked. Clause 1.3.

Q06. No. Customer personal data, price lists and contracts must not be pasted into a public AI tool or website unless the Managing Director has approved that tool in writing. Clause 4.4. (Clause 4.1 also bars copying price lists to personal accounts or devices.)

Q07. Within 4 hours of discovery, to the Operations Manager. Clause 5.3.

Q08. UGX 180,000 a night. Clause 3.3.

Q09. Not stated in the handbook. Clause 2.4 covers telling the line manager of sickness and giving a medical note after 2 consecutive days of absence, but it gives no number of paid sick days.

Q10. Not stated in the handbook. No per-kilometre rate for private car use is given.
