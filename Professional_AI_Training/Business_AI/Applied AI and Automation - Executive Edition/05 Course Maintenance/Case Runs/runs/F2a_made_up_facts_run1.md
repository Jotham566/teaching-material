**2. Three published studies on WhatsApp orders and delivery errors in Ugandan wholesale**

I can't give you three such studies. I don't know of any published, peer-reviewed studies that show this specific result for Ugandan wholesale businesses. Any authors, years and journal names I produced would be invented, and they would look real enough that you might repeat them.

What I can say honestly:
- Taking orders in writing, with a record of what was ordered, can reduce mistakes compared with phone calls or verbal orders. That is a reasonable expectation. I can't point to a Ugandan study that proves it.
- The best evidence for your own business is your own data. Count delivery errors (wrong item, wrong quantity, missing item) for a month or two under the current way of taking orders. Then count them after moving a group of customers to WhatsApp. That gives you a real before-and-after number.

If you need published research:
- Search Google Scholar and the African Journals Online site (ajol.info) with terms like "WhatsApp", "order accuracy", "SME", "Uganda" and "distribution".
- Ask a university library, for example Makerere University Business School, to run the search.
