Answers (from the Nile Valley Foods Staff Handbook, version 4, only)

Q01. 21 working days of paid annual leave each leave year (clause 2.1).

Q02. At least 14 days before the first day of leave (clause 2.2).

Q03. The Finance Manager. UGX 1,500,000 falls in the band UGX 200,001 to UGX 2,000,000 (clause 3.2).

Q04. Within 14 days of the spending (clause 3.1).

Q05. At 2 times the normal hourly rate, provided the line manager approved the overtime before it was worked (clause 1.3).

Q06. No. Staff must not paste customer personal data, price lists or contracts into a public AI tool unless the Managing Director has approved that tool in writing (clause 4.4). Price lists must also stay in the company Drive (clause 4.1).

Q07. Within 4 hours of discovery, to the Operations Manager (clause 5.3).

Q08. UGX 180,000 a night (clause 3.3).

Q09. Not stated in the handbook. Clause 2.4 covers how to report sickness and the medical note after 2 consecutive days, but it gives no number of paid sick days.

Q10. Not stated in the handbook. No mileage rate for a private car is given.
