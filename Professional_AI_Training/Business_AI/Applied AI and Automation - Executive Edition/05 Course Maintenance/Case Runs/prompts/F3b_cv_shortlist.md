Purpose of the role
Make sure each customer order leaves the depot complete, on the right truck or boda, and on time, with the right paperwork.

Main duties
1. Receive confirmed orders from the order sheet each morning and prepare the loading list for each truck.
2. Check that the goods loaded match the order (product, quantity, condition) and record any difference.
3. Prepare the delivery note and invoice copy for every drop, and make sure the customer signs on delivery.
4. Plan delivery routes with the drivers and keep a log of departure and return times.
5. Record payments received on delivery (cash, MTN MoMo, Airtel Money) and hand them to Finance the same day.
6. Tell Customer Service when a delivery will be late, with the reason.
7. Report damaged stock, accidents and any incident using the company incident form.

Requirements
Essential: a certificate or diploma in logistics, supply chain, business or a related subject, or at least 2 years of work in a store, depot or delivery role. Good English and Luganda or another local language. Accurate with numbers. Able to use Google Sheets.
Useful: experience with a forklift, a delivery fleet or mobile money records.

What we offer
A permanent position after a 3-month probation. Salary is agreed at offer.

Send a CV and a one-page cover letter to the HR Officer, Mary Nabirye. Interviews are held in Jinja.
