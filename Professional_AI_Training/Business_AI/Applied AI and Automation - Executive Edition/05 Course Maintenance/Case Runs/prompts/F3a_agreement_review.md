SUPPLY AGREEMENT FOR COOKING OIL

Made on 1 March 2026 between the two parties named in clause 1.

1. Parties
Nile Valley Foods Ltd, Plot 12 Industrial Road, Jinja, Uganda ("NVF"), and Busoga Oils Ltd, Plot 5 Factory Road, Jinja, Uganda ("the Supplier").

2. Goods
The Supplier sells and NVF buys refined cooking oil in 20 litre jerrycans and 200 litre drums, as listed in Schedule 1 (the price list). The oil must match the description on the label and be fit for sale to the public.

3. Price and price adjustment
Prices are in Ugandan shillings and exclude VAT. The Supplier may change any price at its sole discretion by telling NVF in writing, and the new price applies to every order not yet delivered, including orders NVF has already confirmed.

4. Ordering and delivery
NVF orders by email or WhatsApp. The Supplier confirms each order within one working day and delivers to the NVF depot in Jinja within 3 working days of confirmation. Risk in the goods passes to NVF when the goods are signed for at the depot.

5. Payment
The Supplier sends an invoice with each delivery. NVF pays each correct invoice within 30 days of receiving it, by bank transfer or mobile money.

6. Late payment interest
If NVF does not pay an undisputed amount on time, NVF pays simple interest on it at 1% a month from the due date until the day it is paid. The Supplier pays the same rate on any refund or credit it delays.

7. Quality and returns
NVF may reject goods that are damaged, contaminated or different from the order by telling the Supplier within 7 days of delivery. The Supplier collects rejected goods and, within 14 days, replaces them or credits NVF in full.

8. Term and automatic renewal
This agreement starts on 1 March 2026 and runs for 12 months. It then renews automatically for further periods of 12 months unless one party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the current period.

9. Termination
Either party may end this agreement by written notice if the other commits a material breach and does not fix it within 14 days of being told. Either party may also end it at once if the other becomes insolvent.

10. Liability
The total liability of the Supplier to NVF under or in connection with this agreement, for any cause, is limited to the fees NVF paid in the 3 months before the claim arose. This limit applies to every kind of claim, including claims about contaminated or unsafe goods.

11. Confidentiality and personal data
Each party keeps the other's confidential information secret during this agreement and for 3 years after it ends. If the Supplier receives personal data from NVF, such as delivery contacts, it uses that data only on NVF's written instructions, protects it as the Data Protection and Privacy Act 2019 requires, and tells NVF within 72 hours of learning of any loss or leak.

12. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the laws of Uganda, and the courts of Uganda decide any dispute about it.
