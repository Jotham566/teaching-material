REGIONAL DEMAND BRIEF, October (invented publisher: Great Lakes Trade Digest)
Topic: demand for staples among shops and supermarkets in Uganda and western Kenya

Demand
- Shop owners in Eastern Uganda say sales of maize flour and rice rose by about 8% in October compared with September, helped by school feeding orders.
- Supermarkets report that cooking oil in 5 litre bottles is selling faster than 20 litre jerrycans.
- Mobile money use is growing among small shops, and many now pay suppliers on delivery instead of 30 days later.

Buyer behaviour
- Several shops buy smaller quantities more often because of limited storage and cash.
- Buyers say reliable delivery days matter as much as price.

Outlook
- Demand is expected to rise again in December for festive meals. Distributors that run out of sugar and oil in that month risk losing shops to rivals.
