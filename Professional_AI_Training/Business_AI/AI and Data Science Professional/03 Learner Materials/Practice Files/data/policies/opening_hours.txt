Lakeside Mart Opening Hours. Branches open from 7 am to 9 pm Monday to Saturday and from 9 am to 6
pm on Sunday. Kampala Central and Kampala Ntinda close at 10 pm on Fridays. On public holidays all
branches open from 9 am to 5 pm, except on Christmas Day when all branches are closed. The pharmacy
counter in Kampala Central and Entebbe opens from 8 am to 8 pm. Opening hours change during
stocktake, which is announced on the notice board a week before.
