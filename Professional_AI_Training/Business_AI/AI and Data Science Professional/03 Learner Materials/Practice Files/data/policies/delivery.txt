Lakeside Mart Delivery. Delivery is available from Kampala Central, Kampala Ntinda, Entebbe and
Jinja for orders above UGX 80,000. The fee is UGX 5,000 within 5 kilometres and UGX 10,000 within 15
kilometres. Gold loyalty customers receive free delivery within 5 kilometres. Orders placed before 2
pm are delivered the same day. Perishable goods are delivered in cooler boxes. A customer who is not
present at the address is called twice before the order is returned to the branch. Delivery is not
available in Gulu, Mbale, Mbarara or Fort Portal.
