Lakeside Mart Mobile Money Payments. Customers can pay with MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money at all
eight branches. A payment request is sent to the customer's phone and must be approved with the
customer's PIN. If the money leaves the wallet but the till shows no payment, the customer should
keep the transaction message and ask the branch manager, who checks the daily settlement report.
Failed payments are reversed by the provider, usually within 24 hours. Lakeside Mart never asks for
a customer's PIN. Mobile money limits are set by the provider.
