Lakeside Mart Supplier Quality. Suppliers must deliver fresh goods with a delivery note and a
temperature record. A branch manager inspects every delivery. If more than 5 percent of a delivery
is damaged or spoiled, the delivery is rejected and the supplier is told the same day. A supplier
with three rejected deliveries in a quarter is placed on review for 60 days. Complaints from
customers about a supplier's product are logged by item and branch and reviewed monthly by the
buyer. Payment to suppliers is made within 30 days of an accepted delivery.
