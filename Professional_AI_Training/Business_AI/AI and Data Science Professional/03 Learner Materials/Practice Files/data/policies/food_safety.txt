Lakeside Mart Food Safety. Fresh produce is checked each morning and removed if spoiled. Dairy is
stored below 5 degrees Celsius and the fridge temperature is recorded every 4 hours. Products past
their expiry date are removed from shelves daily and logged. A customer who finds an expired item
should return it with the receipt for a full refund and the branch records the incident. Repeated
incidents at a branch are reported to the head office within 48 hours. Staff handling food wash
their hands and wear clean aprons.
