Lakeside Mart Loyalty Programme. Every customer who gives a phone number at the till joins as Basic.
A customer earns 1 point for every UGX 1,000 spent. Silver status starts at 2,000 points in 12
months and Gold at 6,000 points. Silver customers receive 3 percent off selected household items on
Saturdays. Gold customers receive 5 percent off, free delivery within 5 kilometres and early notice
of offers. Points expire after 12 months. Points are added within one day of purchase. Points cannot
be exchanged for cash.
