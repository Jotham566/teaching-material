Lakeside Mart Customer Data. Lakeside Mart collects a customer's phone number, name, age band and
purchases to run the loyalty programme. This follows the principles in the Uganda Data Protection
and Privacy Act, 2019, including collecting only what is needed and keeping it secure. A customer
may ask to see, correct or delete their data by writing to the data officer. Data is kept for 3
years after the last purchase. Analysts working with the data use customer numbers and not names,
and share only summaries outside the company. Customer data is never sold.
