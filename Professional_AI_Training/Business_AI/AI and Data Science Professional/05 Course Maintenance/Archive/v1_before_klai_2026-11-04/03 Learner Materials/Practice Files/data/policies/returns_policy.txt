Lakeside Mart Returns Policy. Customers may return an item within 7 days of purchase with the till
receipt. Fresh produce and bread can be returned only on the day of purchase and only if damaged or
spoiled. Electronics and phones may be exchanged within 14 days if the sealed packaging is intact,
and a repair is offered after that under the manufacturer warranty. Refunds are made to the same
payment method. Mobile money refunds take up to 3 working days. Items bought on a promotion can be
exchanged but not refunded in cash. Opened personal care products cannot be returned for hygiene
reasons.
