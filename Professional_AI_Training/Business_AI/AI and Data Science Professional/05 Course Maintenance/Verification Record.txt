Every deck, figure, the roadmap and the Word documents now use one ink colour (1F2328), a warm grey body (3B4046), hairline rules (C9C5BC) and one clay accent (B04A2B) used only for what matters (checkpoints, errors, the highlighted column or cell). CHECKED, AND HOW
Slides. Every slide was built by an engine that measures text with metric-compatible fonts and refuses to build a slide whose text does not fit at its minimum size (body text 15 points or more, table cells 14, code 14). All 24 decks pass the PowerPoint schema validator, including the animation XML. A contact sheet of every session was rendered in LibreOffice and inspected, and defects found there were fixed (long tokens breaking mid-word, code that did not fit beside its notes, wide figures too small).
Speaker notes. Every slide has a script to read aloud. A lint rejects stage directions, the word click, dashes, markdown, links, banned words and wrong paragraph counts (an opening paragraph plus one per click). Repeated pause lines are flagged. Stock sentences are limited across the course.
Code on slides and in labs. Every pandas, NumPy, matplotlib, seaborn and SQL block was executed against the generated data and its printed output compared with the slide. Lab solution notebooks run top to bottom: run_all_labs.py with --allow-missing-libs reports all 20 coding notebooks OK here, with the cells that need scikit-learn, SciPy, statsmodels, sentence-transformers, transformers, joblib or Streamlit skipped. (On 29 October the runner wrongly failed notebook 18 because a failed assert that depended on a skipped scikit-learn cell was not treated as a skip. It now is.)
Data. The generator asserts the properties the lessons rely on (skewed order values, December peak, the rate-versus-count reversal, a detectable offer lift, correct regression signs, a loan model of honest difficulty with one leakage column, a rain lag in malaria cases). All numbers in the slides and labs trace to the data or answer_key.json.
Facts. Eight independent reviewers each took three sessions and checked every number, definition and API call against the data and the documented libraries. About 30 errors were fixed (for example a p-value described as proof the gap is real, a wrong cross-reference, a Wald bomber story presented as fact, a figure that drew eight of twelve months). Their unverified items are listed below.
Course. Each vocabulary term is defined once. Figure names exist. Document numbers are generated from the same sources as the decks.
