CHANGE OF 4 NOVEMBER 2026 (LATER): NO ASSESSMENT
Lecturer decision: none of the KLAi professional programmes has an exam or any assessment. No marks, grades, pass marks, weights, scoring rubrics, graded checkpoints or graded capstone. Learners build practical projects and keep them. The notebook OK / NOT YET checks, recall questions and exit-check questions stay as self-checks that nobody marks. The certificate is a certificate of completion from KLAi Professional Learning, given for completing the course project, with no other criteria.
