AI AND DATA SCIENCE PROFESSIONAL (KLAi edition)
24 sessions of 2 hours | 48 hours | free tools only | Windows or Mac, in a browser

For working professionals who are confident in Excel or Google Sheets, with no code and no statistics. They finish able to take a business question from raw data to a tested model or analysis, explain it to a manager, and run it as a small app. All course data is fictional and generated (Lakeside Mart retail, Nile Microfinance loans, District Clinics, customer feedback messages; Uganda; UGX).

Every session has the same shape: recall, then concepts with laptops closed (picture, plain words, one example by hand), a break, then one switch to the notebook or the sheet for a lab (predict, run, read, change), then an exit check. Concepts are named with the real industry term and a plain explanation beside it.

BEFORE THE FIRST CLASS
1 Upload data.zip to Google Drive and share it with 'Anyone with the link'. Paste the link on one line in KLAi Build/data_zip_url.txt, then regenerate the notebooks (python3 kit/make_notebooks.py N for each coding session). Until then every learner uploads data.zip by hand.
2 Open each notebook in Google Colab and choose Runtime, Restart session and run all (Sessions 12 to 18, 20 and 23 have never run on the real libraries; see the Verification Record).
3 Confirm the wording on the certificate of completion (given for completing the course project, no other criteria).
4 Read the notes of each session aloud once.
