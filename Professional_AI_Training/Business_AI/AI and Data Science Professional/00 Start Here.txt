AI AND DATA SCIENCE PROFESSIONAL
24 sessions of 2 hours | 48 hours | free tools only | Windows or Mac, in a browser

For working professionals who are confident in Excel or Google Sheets, with no code and no statistics. They finish able to take a business question from raw data to a tested model or analysis, explain it to a manager, and run it as a small app.

01 Course Overview
Student Roadmap (one page).pdf is the page to share with students. The .pptx beside it is the editable version.

02 Class Slides
One PowerPoint per session, numbered in teaching order. 545 slides in all: 320 are animated (895 click reveals), and every slide has speaker notes written as a script to read aloud. Play the animated slides in Slide Show mode; editing view shows the finished diagram.

03 Learner Materials
Student Setup Guide.docx: what to do before Session 1. Send it before the course starts.
Sheets Lab Sheets.docx: the task lists for Sessions 1 to 4 (Google Sheets) and the presentation preparation in Session 24. Hand out in Session 1.
Glossary.docx: all 161 vocabulary terms, each with the spreadsheet idea it matches. Hand out in Session 1.
Assessment and Capstone Guide.docx: how the marks are made, the three checkpoints, the capstone steps, the presentation and its rubric. Hand out in Session 1.
Practice Files/data: the generated data (see DATA DICTIONARY.txt), lakeside.db (SQLite) and lakeside_for_sheets.xlsx. Practice Files/data.zip is the same set for students to upload.
Practice Files/notebooks: the student lab notebook for each coding session. Open them in Google Colab.

04 Facilitator Materials
Course Plan and Tutor Guide.docx: the session shape, all 24 sessions, assessment, tools, data, what to do before the course starts, and what is in each folder.
Sheets Lab Answers.docx: the answers to the Sheets tasks, with the numbers from the 2,000-order workbook. Tutor only; do not hand out.
Benchmark and Design Report.docx: how the course compares with roadmap.sh and the Google, IBM, DeepLearning.AI and free courses, what it leaves out, tool licences, the sources, and what is and is not verified.
Lab Solutions: the solution notebook for each coding session (tutor only).
run_all_labs.py: runs every solution notebook top to bottom. Run it once before you teach.
Playback and Rehearsal Checklist.txt: every animated slide with its click count, and the demonstrations that need your own accounts.

05 Course Maintenance
Authoring: everything needed to rebuild the decks, notebooks, data, figures and documents. Read Authoring/README - Read First.txt.
Research/Research Notes.txt: where the benchmark research is kept.
Verification Record.txt: what was checked, what could not be, and what you must do before the first class.
Authoring/REVIEW BRIEF.md and AUTHOR BRIEF.md: the instructions the sessions were written and reviewed under.

BEFORE THE FIRST CLASS
1 Upload data.zip somewhere students can reach it.
2 Run run_all_labs.py in 04 Facilitator Materials (or open each solution notebook in Colab and choose Run all). The authoring machine could not run the scikit-learn, SciPy, statsmodels, sentence-transformers or Streamlit cells.
3 Play every animated slide in PowerPoint.
4 Read the notes aloud once per session.
