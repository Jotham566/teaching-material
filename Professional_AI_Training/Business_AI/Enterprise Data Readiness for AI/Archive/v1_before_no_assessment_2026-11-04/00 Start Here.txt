ENTERPRISE DATA READINESS FOR AI
12 sessions of 2 hours | 24 hours | free tools only | for a decision maker and a data or IT colleague from the same organisation

A leader and a data or IT colleague from one organisation. No coding needed for leaders. Teams should be comfortable with spreadsheets and databases. They finish with a scored baseline, a tested search over the organisation's own documents and a twelve-month plan presented as a board paper. Delivery is either a cohort of organisations or in house for one organisation. Pairing a decision maker with a data colleague is preferred. KLAi issues no certificate for the course.

STATUS: built. 276 slides, 12 team notebooks with solutions, the Kitara Group case data, the workpiece templates and the handouts exist. See 05 Course Maintenance/Verification Record.txt for what is and is not checked.

BEFORE THE FIRST CLASS
1 Upload data.zip where participants can reach it.
2 Send the Setup Guide and ask each participant to run the first notebook cell in Colab.
3 Run run_all_labs.py in 04 Facilitator Materials, then run the heavy-library cells once in Colab (the Playback and Rehearsal Checklist lists them).
4 Play every animated slide in PowerPoint.
5 Read the notes aloud once per session.
6 Collect from each participant the written approval that their 50 documents are non-confidential (needed by Session 5).
7 Read the primary sources listed in section 10 of the Benchmark and Design Report.
